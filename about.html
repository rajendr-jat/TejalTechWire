About TejalTechWire

TejalTechWire is an independent news desk covering artificial intelligence, technology, and gadgets for readers in the US and UK. We publish concise, plain-language updates on the developments shaping the tech industry — written so anyone can follow along, not just industry insiders.

Our goal is simple: help everyday readers understand what's happening in tech without wading through jargon or dense analyst-speak.

Our Editorial Process

Every story on TejalTechWire is reviewed and edited by a human before it is published. We use AI as a tool to help us gather information from multiple established technology publications and prepare an initial draft quickly — but nothing goes live automatically. A member of our team reads each draft, checks it, edits it where needed, and only then approves it for publishing.

Put simply: AI helps us get news to you faster, but a human always reviews and signs off on what you read.

We believe in being upfront about how our content is made. If you notice any factual error, please get in touch via our Contact page and we'll review it promptly.

What We Cover

Artificial Intelligence, general Technology news, Gadgets, Mobile/Smartphones, and Business & Big Tech. New updates are published regularly throughout the day.
