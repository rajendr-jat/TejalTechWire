TITLE: Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra Is Now Available In India Through Official Certified Program

CONTENT:
Samsung is giving shoppers a new way to buy its top-tier smartphones. The tech giant has just made the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra and standard Galaxy S26 available in India as part of its official Certified Re-Newed program. This setup lets you buy official, refurbished phones directly from the manufacturer for a much lower price.

These are not just any used phones. Samsung takes pre-owned devices and thoroughly inspects, cleans, and tests them to make sure they meet strict quality standards. The company even swaps out old parts with genuine replacements and puts in a brand-new battery.

HEADING: What You Get for Your Money

Every device in this program comes completely reset to its factory settings with all previous personal data wiped. Samsung also installs the very latest software updates before shipping the phone out. To give buyers peace of mind, Samsung includes a 1-year warranty with every purchase. The company says these phones look and feel like new, but they are sold at up to 85% of their original launch prices.

HEADING: Pricing and Models Offered

Buyers can choose between different storage options for both the standard and Ultra models. The basic Galaxy S26 with 256 gigabytes of storage starts at 74,799 Indian Rupees, which is a discount from its original launch price of 87,999 Rupees. If you want the top-end Galaxy S26 Ultra with 512 gigabytes of storage, it will cost 135,999 Rupees, down from the original launch price of 159,999 Rupees. These devices are sold directly on the official Samsung website and their retail app.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is a certified re-newed phone?

A: It is a used phone that Samsung has officially inspected, cleaned, repaired with genuine parts, and fitted with a brand-new battery.

Q: Do these refurbished phones come with a warranty?

A: Yes, Samsung provides a full 1-year warranty with every refurbished phone sold through this program.

Q: Where can you buy these refurbished Samsung devices?

A: You can purchase them directly through Samsung's official website and the Samsung Shop mobile app in India.
