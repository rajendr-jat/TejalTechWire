TITLE: Motorola Unveils New Edge Phone, Watch Ultra, And Swarovski Razr

CONTENT:
Motorola has just showed off its latest line of high-tech gadgets at a major European technology show. The big announcements include a brand-new smartphone with a massive battery, a premium smartwatch powered by Google software, and a sparkly, limited-edition folding phone. 

When Motorola unveils these new devices for 2026, the company is aiming to combine fashion with top-tier technology. They have teamed up with famous color experts at Pantone and luxury crystal brand Swarovski to make these gadgets look as good as they perform.

HEADING: The Motorola Edge 70 Plus Phone

The new Edge 70 Plus smartphone features a smooth, fully curved design on both the front and back so it fits comfortably in your hand. It comes with a beautiful 6.78-inch screen and a massive 200-megapixel main camera, which is one of the highest resolutions you can find on any phone. To keep you going all day, it packs a giant 6,500mAh battery that charges quickly, plus it has a special mode that lets you play games using plugged-in power without draining the battery life.

HEADING: The New Motorola Watch Ultra

If you want a powerful wearable, the Watch Ultra is a premium 46mm smartwatch that runs on Wear OS by Google. It features a super bright screen and built-in cellular data, meaning you can make calls and use Google Pay even if you leave your phone at home. Motorola partnered with the fitness experts at Polar to build in professional-grade heart rate tracking, running coaches, and deep sleep analysis.

HEADING: A Sparkly Swarovski Folding Phone

For those who love fashion, Motorola has also created a special edition of its folding phone, called the Razr 2026 in the US and the Razr 70 elsewhere. This eye-catching device is covered in a soft, quilted material and decorated with 35 hand-placed Swarovski crystals. It is designed to look like a piece of high-end jewelry while still offering all the modern features of a premium folding smartphone.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When will the Motorola Watch Ultra be available?
A: The smartwatch will roll out to North America, Europe, the Middle East, Latin America, and Asia Pacific in the coming weeks.

Q: What is special about the battery in the Edge 70 Plus?
A: It has a massive 6,500mAh battery that supports 45W fast charging and bypass charging to power gaming sessions directly.

Q: Does the new Motorola Watch Ultra require a phone to work?
A: No, this watch has built-in LTE cellular connectivity, so it can make calls and operate completely on its own.
