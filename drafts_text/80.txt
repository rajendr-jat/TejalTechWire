TITLE: Meta Reveals Muse: The New AI Agent That Can Shop and Plan Trips For You

CONTENT:
Meta has announced a brand-new helper for your phone and computer. It is called Muse, and Meta describes it as a personal AI agent. Unlike basic virtual assistants that only answer simple questions, this new tool can actually complete real-world tasks for you.

For example, you can ask Muse to search for flights, book dinner tables, or buy items online. It does all of this on your behalf, meaning you do not have to click through multiple websites yourself. Meta wants this tool to make your daily digital chores much easier.

HEADING: How Muse Works

To use Muse, you do not need any special technical skills. It has its own website and apps, and it connects with other popular services you already use. This includes Google Workspace, Spotify, Ticketmaster, and Apple Health. If you give it permission, Muse can look at recipes you saved on Instagram, make a grocery list from them, compare food prices, and order the ingredients to your house.

HEADING: Privacy and Safety Controls

Giving an AI control over your shopping and emails requires a lot of trust. Because of this, Meta has built several safety rules into Muse. The assistant will always ask for your final approval before it spends your money or sends an email. It also runs in its own private virtual machine, which is a secure digital space, to keep your personal data safe from others. You can also choose to opt out of having your chats used to train Meta's AI models.

HEADING: Where You Will Find It

Meta is planning to put Muse in several different places. You will be able to talk to it through WhatsApp and even use it through Meta's smart glasses. This means you can get help while you are walking around. However, Meta admits that the tool is not perfect and will still make occasional mistakes as it learns.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is Muse?

A: Muse is a new AI assistant from Meta that can handle complex digital tasks like shopping, planning vacations, and sending emails for you.

Q: Is Muse safe to use?

A: Yes, Meta has built in safety systems so Muse must ask for your permission before doing things like spending money or sending emails.

Q: Can Muse connect to other apps?

A: Yes, Muse can connect to services like Google Workspace, Spotify, and Ticketmaster, and it can even build its own connections to other websites.
