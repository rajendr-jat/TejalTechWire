TITLE: Apple's New iPhone Foldables Are Here, But Do They Solve the Big Problems?

CONTENT:
Apple has finally released its very first folding phone, called the iPhone Duo. This is the first major change to the shape of the iPhone in 19 years. Many tech fans are excited, but these new iPhone foldables might not actually solve the biggest problems that have plagued folding screens for years.

Folding phones have been around for about 7 years now, so we already know a lot about how they work. While it is tempting to think Apple has fixed all the usual issues, the reality is quite different. The new device still comes with some major downsides that might make you think twice before buying one.

HEADING: A Very High Price Tag

The iPhone Duo costs $2000 for the basic model. While some rumors suggested it could cost up to $3000, Apple kept it at $2000 to avoid another expensive flop like their Vision Pro headset. Still, $2000 is double the price of a normal high-end phone, and most people will find it hard to justify spending that much money on a device that is essentially just a phone.

HEADING: Not a Real Tablet

Many people buy folding phones because they want a phone and a tablet in one device. However, the inner screen is not actually that much bigger than a standard phone screen when you watch videos. Many apps are also not designed to work well on this square shape. Instead of getting the best of both worlds, you might end up with a screen that feels awkward and fails to replace your iPad.

HEADING: Worse Everyday Phone Features

To make a phone fold in half, manufacturers have to make some big sacrifices. The iPhone Duo is thicker and heavier than a normal phone. It also has shorter battery life, weaker cameras, and worse protection against water and dust. This means the actual phone experience is worse than what you would get from a standard, non-folding iPhone.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much does the iPhone Duo cost?

A: The starting price for the iPhone Duo is $2000.

Q: Is the screen on the iPhone Duo as good as a tablet?

A: No, the inner screen is not much bigger than a regular phone when watching videos, and many apps are not optimized for the folding shape.

Q: What are the main downsides of the iPhone Duo compared to a normal iPhone?

A: It is heavier and thicker, with worse cameras, shorter battery life, and less protection against water and dust.
