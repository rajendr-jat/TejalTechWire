TITLE: TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 brings back the builders stage to help startups scale

CONTENT:
Starting a new business is hard, but growing it into a massive company is even harder. That is why the builders stage is returning to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 to offer real, practical strategies for new business founders. The major event will take place from October 13 to October 15, 2026, at the Moscone Center in San Francisco, where over 10,000 people are expected to gather.

This special stage is designed for startup owners who want to scale up their companies. Industry experts, top investors, and tech leaders will share their personal stories and tips. Attendees can learn how to raise money, hire the best workers, and survive in a world currently obsessed with artificial intelligence (AI), which refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that usually need human intelligence.

HEADING: Surviving the AI Craze

One of the biggest worries for new companies is how to compete with tech giants like OpenAI. The event will feature sessions on how to make your business unique so larger AI firms do not easily replace you. Other talks will focus on how to build a successful company without using AI at all, proving that good business basics still matter.

HEADING: Hiring and Funding Tips

Finding and keeping talented workers is another major hurdle, especially when AI companies are offering massive salaries. Speakers will discuss modern ways to handle pay, company culture, and employee benefits to attract top talent. There will also be sessions on how to win early funding from investors before you even have a finished product ready to sell.

HEADING: Dates and Ticket Deals
