TITLE: This unique startup builds other startups and just raised 100m

CONTENT:
Imagine a company whose entire job is to create other companies. This unique startup builds other startups and just raised 100m to design physical AI for major brands. 

Vantora, which used to be called UP.Labs when it started in 2020, does not build products for everyday shoppers. Instead, it creates brand-new businesses to fix specific issues for massive companies like Porsche and Alaska Airlines. With this new cash injection, Vantora is changing its strategy to help these giant clients keep their new technology completely to themselves.

HEADING: What is physical AI

Physical AI is technology that helps real-world machines work on their own. Instead of just writing text or generating images on a screen, this type of artificial intelligence is put into physical hardware. For example, it can help a shipping company automate its heavy trucks, or help a factory run its assembly line machines without human help. Vantora wants to build startups that specialize in this kind of real-world automation.

HEADING: Keeping secrets from competitors

In the past, Vantora built startups that eventually sold their technology to the wider public. But many big corporations did not like sharing these advanced tools with their rivals. Under Vantora's new model, its corporate partners have the choice to absorb these mini-companies directly into their own businesses. This means a company like Porsche can keep the custom artificial intelligence software completely private, giving them a massive advantage over competitors.

HEADING: A proven track record

Vantora has already launched several successful projects since its start in 2022. Its list of corporate partners includes big names like J.B. Hunt, Wabash, and TDG, the parent company of Ashley Furniture. With $100 million in fresh funding from Silversmith Capital Partners, the firm is ready to tackle even larger industrial challenges without relying on outside venture capital firms.
