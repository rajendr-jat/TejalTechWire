TITLE: Tesla Cybercab Hits The Road But Faces An Immediate Safety Snag

CONTENT:
Tesla recently showed off its brand-new robotaxi, the Cybercab, at a quiet event in Austin, Texas. But just as the first Tesla Cybercab hits the road, the company has run into a major safety snag. 

The US government has already opened an investigation into these self-driving cars. This happened only a few hours after the vehicles started driving on public streets.

HEADING: What is the Cybercab
The Cybercab is a futuristic taxi designed to drive itself without any help from a human. It does not have a steering wheel or any pedals for braking and accelerating. So far, Tesla has registered only 45 of these vehicles in the state of Texas. 

HEADING: Why It is Under Investigation
The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration started looking into the vehicles almost immediately. Current safety laws in the US say that cars must have manual controls like steering wheels and pedals. Tesla chose to self-certify its cars, which means the company itself declared the vehicles were safe. The safety agency wants to make sure these driverless cars are actually safe for the public.

HEADING: A Quiet Launch
Many people expected a massive celebration for the new robotaxi. However, the launch event was surprisingly quiet. Even Tesla boss Elon Musk did not show up, which surprised many investors. This quiet launch and the quick government probe have left some people doubting if Tesla can truly compete with established self-driving taxi services.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Does the Tesla Cybercab have a steering wheel?

A: No, the Cybercab does not have a steering wheel or pedals, because it is designed to drive completely on its own.

Q: Why is the government investigating the Cybercab?

A: The US safety agency is investigating because federal rules require cars to have manual controls, and Tesla chose to self-certify its driverless design.

Q: Can children ride in the Cybercab?

A: No, guides published by Tesla show that this new robotaxi is not designed for children.
