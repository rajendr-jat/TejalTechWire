TITLE: Samsung Galaxy Buds leak reveals cool new clip-on design

CONTENT:
Samsung is working on a brand-new style of wireless earbuds, and a major Samsung Galaxy Buds leak has just given us our best look yet at them. These new clip-on earbuds, reportedly called the Galaxy Buds On, will feature an open-ear design. This means they let you listen to your favorite music while still being able to hear the world around you.

Unlike traditional earbuds that plug deep inside your ear canal, these new ones clip onto the side of your ear. Pictures found inside a Samsung mobile app show how they work and reveal several smart new features.

HEADING: How They Look and Work

The design has 2 main parts. The speaker sits on 1 side, and the other side rests behind your ear. A flexible silicone loop connects the 2 pieces to keep them securely in place. They also have tiny metal charging pins, which means they will charge up normally when you pop them into their carrying case. They will come in at least 2 colors, black and white.

HEADING: Smart Features and Gestures

Because these buds do not block your ears, Samsung is adding smart sound controls. The volume can automatically turn up or down depending on how noisy your surroundings are. You will also be able to control them with simple body movements, like nodding your head. There are even quick shortcut gestures to start a voice recording or launch Samsung's artificial intelligence assistant, Galaxy AI.

HEADING: Release Date Expectations

We do not have an official release date from Samsung yet. Early rumors suggested they might arrive sooner, but experts now expect the company to launch them in the last 3 months of 2026.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What are the Samsung Galaxy Buds On?

A: They are upcoming wireless earbuds from Samsung that clip onto your ear instead of sitting inside your ear canal.

Q: When will the new Galaxy Buds On launch?

A: They are currently expected to launch in the final quarter of 2026.

Q: What colors will they come in?

A: Leaked images confirm they will be available in both black and white.
