TITLE: Apple has finally unfolded the iPhone Duo: Are more foldables on the way?

CONTENT:
Apple has finally taken the plunge into the world of folding screens. Under the leadership of its new CEO, John Ternus, the company recently revealed its first-ever folding phone: the iPhone Duo. This premium device costs $1,999 and shows that Apple is ready to compete directly with other tech giants who have been making folding devices for years.

Now that Apple has unfolded this major new device, tech fans are already wondering what comes next. By proving it can create a strong hinge and a super-thin folding screen, the tech giant has opened the door for even more exciting designs. Experts believe Apple might soon create both smaller and larger foldables to give users more choices.

HEADING: A smaller flip phone could be next

Many people miss smaller, compact phones that easily fit into a pocket or a small bag. A smaller device, which some are calling an "iPhone Flip," would fold down horizontally just like the old-school flip phones from the early 2000s. Other companies like Samsung and Motorola already sell highly popular versions of these smaller flip phones for a fraction of the cost of giant folding phones. A smaller option would be a great way for Apple to satisfy users who want a tiny phone that still offers a big screen when opened.

HEADING: The dream of a folding iPad

Apple could also go big by creating a folding tablet. Imagine a 13-inch iPad Pro that folds in half like a notebook. You could use the top half to watch a video or take a video call, while using the bottom half as a touch-screen keyboard or a digital notepad. While other companies have tried making folding computers, they often struggle with clunky software. Because Apple is known for making smooth, easy-to-use software, a folding iPad could truly change how we work and play on the go.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much does the iPhone Duo cost?

A: The iPhone Duo has a starting price of $1,999.

Q: Who is the new CEO of Apple?

A: John Ternus is the new CEO of Apple, and he introduced the new folding phone at the company's recent launch event.
