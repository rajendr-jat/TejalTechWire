TITLE: Rock Band Muse Just Lost Its Social Media Handles

CONTENT:
Imagine waking up to find your online identity has been taken over by a massive tech company. That is exactly what seems to have happened to the famous British rock band Muse. The band has lost their long-held social media handles on both Instagram and X (formerly Twitter) to Meta's brand-new artificial intelligence assistant, which is also named Muse.

For many years, the rock group used the simple username @muse. However, fans recently noticed that the band's official accounts suddenly changed to @museband. Shortly after this swap, Meta launched its new AI agent (an automated software program that can perform tasks for users) and immediately began using the prized @muse handle.

HEADING: A History of Taking Usernames

This is not the only time Meta has taken over desirable usernames right before a major product launch. In 2021, the company took the @Meta handle on Instagram from an independent motorcycle magazine after changing its corporate name from Facebook. In 2023, Meta also took over the @threads handle, which was previously owned by a small fashion brand. The tech giant rarely explains how these handovers happen, and its official rules actually ban users from buying or selling accounts.

HEADING: An Ironic Twist for Fans

Supporters of the rock group find the situation highly ironic. The band has been writing songs about the dangers of technology and corporate control since they trademarked their name in 1999. Many fans have shared their disappointment online about a corporate AI tool taking over the band's original identity. Even Meta executives seemed confused by the sudden change, as some of their early posts promoting the new AI assistant mistakenly tagged the band's new account instead.

HEADING: Similar Moves on Other Platforms

Meta is not alone in reclaiming coveted usernames for its own purposes. The platform X has also taken accounts from long-time users without their permission. In 2023, the original owner of the @X handle was forced to give it up to Elon Musk in exchange for some company merchandise. Later that same year, the user who held the @music handle since 2007 also lost their username to the platform.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is Meta's new Muse?

A: Muse is the name of Meta's newly released artificial intelligence assistant, also known as an AI agent.

Q: Did the band Muse sell their usernames?

A: It is not clear if the band was paid or if Meta took the handles, as neither Meta nor the band's representatives have commented on the matter.

Q: What are the band's new social media accounts?

A: The rock band now uses the handle @museband on both Instagram and X.
