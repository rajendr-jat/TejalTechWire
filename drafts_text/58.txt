TITLE: What will Apple under new boss John Ternus look like?

CONTENT:
Apple has officially entered a brand-new era. Tim Cook has stepped down from his role as chief executive officer, passing the torch to the former hardware chief, John Ternus. This massive shift leaves many tech fans wondering what the future of Apple under John Ternus will look like. 

Even though Cook is stepping aside as the main boss, he is not leaving the tech giant completely. He will transition into a new role as executive chairman, which means he will still be around to handle big-picture relationships and political matters. Meanwhile, the incoming leader has to get to work immediately, with a massive product event already scheduled for next week.

HEADING: A Very Busy First Week

The transition is happening fast, and the new leader is jumping straight into the deep end. In his very first message to staff, he teased a major product launch scheduled for next week. This means his first big task will be overseeing a major public event, likely featuring the latest iPhones, before he even has time to settle into his new office.

HEADING: What Tim Cook Will Do Now

Although he is no longer running the day-to-day operations, Tim Cook will still play a major role at the company. As executive chairman, Cook will focus on managing relationships with governments and partners around the world. This allows the new chief executive to focus on products and internal operations without getting bogged down in political debates.

HEADING: The Shift to Software and AI

While the new boss spent years leading the hardware team, his biggest challenge might actually be software. Tech experts point out that we are now in the age of artificial intelligence, or AI. The company will need to balance its famous physical designs with smart, new software features to keep up with competitors in 2025 and beyond.
