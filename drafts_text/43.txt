TITLE: TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 features brand new stage for real world AI and robots

CONTENT:
The big tech conference TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is introducing a brand-new space this year. It is called the Real World AI Stage. This new addition will focus on how artificial intelligence is moving out of our computer screens and into physical reality. This includes everything from advanced robots to bringing back extinct animals.

The event will take place from October 13 to October 15 at the Moscone West convention center in San Francisco. It will bring together top experts from major companies like Nvidia to talk about how physical machines are learning to think and act on their own.

HEADING: The Robot Data Problem

Right now, smart computer programs like ChatGPT learned by reading billions of words on the internet. But physical robots do not have a massive library of real-world movement data to learn from. Nvidia and other tech startups are trying to fix this. They are building virtual simulation worlds and data pipelines to teach robots how to move and navigate.

HEADING: High Stakes and De-Extinction

When artificial intelligence controls physical machines, safety is incredibly important. A simple computer glitch could crash a drone, a military aircraft, or a self-driving car. Speakers at the event will discuss how to build trustworthy systems. Attendees will also hear from Colossal Biosciences, a company using advanced biology and technology to try to bring extinct animal species back to life.

HEADING: AI Without an Internet Connection

Most AI tools today rely on the cloud, which is a network of massive remote computer servers. But machines in space, on battlefields, or in remote industrial sites must work without any internet connection. Experts at the event will share how they build powerful AI that runs directly on small, local devices so they never fail when they are offline.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When and where is TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 taking place?

A: The event will run from October 13 to October 15 at Moscone West in San Francisco.

Q: What is the main focus of the Real World AI Stage?

A: It is a new stage dedicated to how artificial intelligence connects with physical hardware like robots, defense systems, and biology.

Q: Which major companies are participating in this new stage?

A: Leaders from top technology firms will speak, including Nvidia, Colossal Biosciences, and Shield AI.
