TITLE: A secret Elizabeth Holmes documentary stuns film festival crowds

CONTENT:
A secret Elizabeth Holmes documentary stuns moviegoers at a major film festival. Audiences at the Telluride film festival thought they were watching a mystery movie. Instead, they got a surprise look at the final days of the infamous Theranos founder before she went to prison.

The new movie is called "You Can See Everything". It was filmed in secret by directors Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim. They got incredible access to Holmes and her husband right before her prison sentence started.

HEADING: What the Film is About

The documentary follows Elizabeth Holmes and her husband, Billy Evans, during the last 34 days before she entered federal prison in May 2023. Holmes is currently serving an 11-year sentence for tricking investors about her blood-testing technology. One of the directors even lived in the couple's guest house to film their daily lives. The movie also features a famous actress who ends up becoming part of the story.

HEADING: When Can You Watch It

The entertainment company A24 is scheduled to release the film to the public in October. This release comes as Holmes continues her legal battles from behind bars. Her appeals to change her sentence were rejected, and she is now waiting to see if her request for a presidential commutation gets approved.
