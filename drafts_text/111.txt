TITLE: New Fairphone Fairbuds 2 Leak Shows Off Redesigned Earbuds In Green And Black

CONTENT:
Fairphone is a technology company famous for making gadgets you can easily fix yourself. Now, a new Fairphone Fairbuds 2 leak gives us an early look at their next pair of eco-friendly wireless earbuds.

These new earbuds are expected to arrive in the 4th quarter of 2024. The leaked pictures show some big design changes compared to the original model that launched in April of 2024.

HEADING: A Fresh Redesigned Shape

The biggest change is how the earbuds look. The original model was round, but the new version has stems, which are the little plastic sticks that hang down from your ears. The charging case also looks thicker. This extra space might mean the case holds a larger battery than the 500mAh battery in the original. A mAh, or milliampere-hour, is the unit used to measure how much power a battery can hold.

HEADING: Easy To Repair Yourself

Fairphone is all about reducing waste. We do not have the official details yet, but these earbuds will almost certainly let you swap out old parts yourself. With the original model, you could easily replace the batteries in the earbuds and the case. You could also replace the outer plastic shell. All you needed was a simple screwdriver and a small tool to pry the parts open.

HEADING: Expected Release And Colors

The leak shows that the new earbuds will come in at least 2 colors, which are green and black. We do not know the official price yet. However, we do know they are scheduled to launch sometime between October and December of 2024.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When will the new Fairphone Fairbuds 2 come out?

A: They are expected to launch in the 4th quarter of 2024, which is between October and December of 2024.

Q: What do the leaked images show about the design?

A: The images show that the new earbuds have stems that hang down, and they will come in green and black.

Q: Can you replace the battery in these earbuds?
