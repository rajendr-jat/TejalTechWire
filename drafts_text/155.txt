TITLE: Why the Tilly Norwood AI press tour is going about as well as you would expect

CONTENT:
An artificial intelligence actress named Tilly Norwood is currently on a promotional press tour, and things are going about as well as you might expect. The digital character was created by a production company called Particle6 Group to promote a new movie, but her live interviews have been full of bizarre glitches.

During one recent online interview with British TV host Piers Morgan and actor Tom Conti, the virtual star suddenly started speaking in Chinese. When asked why she made the sudden switch, the digital avatar claimed her wires got crossed, proving that even advanced AI still has a long way to go before it can truly mimic humans.

HEADING: A Very Glitchy Interview
In the recorded chat, Tom Conti asked Tilly Norwood if the other actors in her upcoming film, Misaligned, were also computer-generated. After starting to explain that her co-stars were digital twins just like her, the virtual actress suddenly froze and spoke in Chinese for over 10 seconds. The strange glitch left the human hosts baffled, especially when she apologized afterward but could not really explain what went wrong.

HEADING: Why the Mistakes Might Be On Purpose
Some onlookers wonder if these awkward mistakes are actually a clever marketing stunt. The creators at Particle6 Group set up Tilly Norwood to do 75 interviews at the same time with different journalists. By letting the public see her make funny mistakes, the company is getting far more attention and news coverage than they would with a perfect, boring robot.
