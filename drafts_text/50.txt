TITLE: Google Releases New AI Weather Model That Uses Live Satellite Data

CONTENT:
Google has launched a brand-new artificial intelligence system to make weather forecasts much more accurate. This new Google weather model uses live satellite data to predict rain, wind, and temperature with incredibly high detail. Instead of waiting hours for updates, this smart system looks at the sky in real time to tell us exactly what is happening right now.

Developed by Google DeepMind, the technology is called WeatherNext 3. It is designed to help everyday people plan their day, but it is also a massive help for green energy companies. By tracking wind speeds and sunlight levels, it can help wind and solar farms generate electricity more efficiently.

HEADING: How the New Model Works

Older weather models usually had a 6-hour delay because they relied on slow computer simulations. WeatherNext 3 fixes this by pulling information directly from space satellites and local weather stations. It zooms in on tiny areas, measuring weather in 5-kilometer blocks instead of the old 25-kilometer blocks. This means it can spot sudden changes in humidity and temperature from one neighborhood to the next.

HEADING: Better Rain Predictions and Where to Find It

By mixing satellite analysis from Google and NASA, this system is 50 percent more accurate at predicting rain and snow. It will soon power the weather forecasts you see inside Google Search, the Gemini app, and Google Maps. If you want to try it out today, you can test the technology yourself online in the Google Weather Lab.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Where can I try this new AI weather tool?

A: You can test it right now by visiting the Google Weather Lab online.

Q: How much more accurate is the new model at predicting rain?

A: It is up to 50 percent more accurate at predicting rain and snow compared to older methods.
