TITLE: Apple Nailed the New iPhone Duo Split Screen Multitasking

CONTENT:
Apple recently showed off its very first foldable phone, called the iPhone Duo. It is an exciting new gadget, and tech fans are already loving 1 specific feature. Apple nailed the new iPhone Duo split screen multitasking, making it incredibly easy to run 2 apps at the same time.

Multitasking means using more than 1 app at once on your screen. On the new foldable iPhone, this feels smooth and simple. However, this great new feature is also making some people look at the iPad and wonder why its system feels so left behind.

HEADING: How the iPhone Duo Does It
On the iPhone Duo, you can easily slide an app to occupy 1 half of the screen. While that app sits on 1 side, you can still see your normal home screen on the other side to choose your second app. You can even pair 2 apps together so they always open at the same time. Plus, you can watch a video on 1 half of the screen while typing or browsing on the other half.

HEADING: Why the iPad is Lagging Behind
Many tablet users find the iPad multitasking experience to be far too convoluted. Convoluted means overly complicated and difficult to follow. Recent software updates like iPadOS 26 and iPadOS 27 force users into a complex windowing system, which lets you resize and overlap apps like on a desktop computer. This feels heavy and difficult to use, especially on smaller tablets like the iPad mini. It is frustrating because the iPad mini is almost the same size as the unfolded iPhone Duo, yet it lacks the simpler screen options.

HEADING: What Apple Should Do Next
The technology under the hood of the iPad is very similar to the iPhone. Since Apple has already created this simpler, elegant way to split the screen on its new foldable phone, it should bring the same feature to the iPad. Giving tablet users a simpler option would make the iPad much friendlier for everyday tasks, especially for people who do not use an external keyboard.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is the iPhone Duo?
A: It is Apple's very first foldable smartphone that can open up to reveal a larger screen.

Q: Can you run 2 apps at once on the iPhone Duo?
A: Yes, you can easily split the screen to use 2 apps side by side and even save your favorite app pairs.

Q: Why are users unhappy with iPad multitasking?
A: Many find the current iPad software convoluted because it forces users to manage overlapping windows instead of offering simple split screens.
