TITLE: How the New iPhone and Samsung Galaxy Compete for the Best Giant Phone

CONTENT:
Big phones are more popular than ever, and the 2 biggest names in tech have just put out their latest giant models. Apple has launched the iPhone 18 Pro Max, while Samsung is pushing its Galaxy S26 Ultra. If you want a top-tier phone with a massive screen but do not want a folding device, these are your 2 main choices. We will look at how the latest iPhone and Galaxy models compete to see which one is right for your pocket.

At first glance, these 2 massive devices look quite similar. They both feature huge 6.9-inch screens and classic rectangular designs. However, when you look closer, there are some very important differences in how they feel, how bright their screens get, and how their cameras take photos.

HEADING: Size, Weight, and Screen Differences
The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is surprisingly lighter and thinner than Apple's giant phone. The Galaxy weighs 214 grams and is 7.9 mm thick, while the iPhone 18 Pro Max weighs 249 grams and is 8.75 mm thick. A lighter phone is much easier to hold for long periods. Both phones have incredibly sharp displays with a 120Hz refresh rate, which is how many times the screen updates its image every second to make scrolling look smooth. However, the iPhone screen can reach 3,000 nits of brightness. Nits measure how bright a screen can get, and a higher number means the iPhone is easier to read in direct, bright sunlight compared to the Galaxy at 2,600 nits.

HEADING: Stylus Support and Camera Features
One big bonus for Samsung fans is the built-in S Pen stylus, which lets you write notes and draw directly on the screen. To use a stylus on an Apple device, you would have to buy the far more expensive iPhone Duo. When it comes to cameras, both of these phones are outstanding. The iPhone 18 Pro Max has 3 48-megapixel cameras, including a new main lens with a variable aperture. This is a mechanical feature that lets the camera lens open and close physically to control the light, just like a professional camera. On the other hand, the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra boasts a massive 200-megapixel main camera, which lets you crop into photos without losing detail.

HEADING: Choosing the Right Giant Phone
Ultimately, your choice will likely come down to which software system you already use. If you love the freedom of Android and want a lighter phone with a built-in stylus, the Galaxy is the clear winner. But if you prefer Apple's iOS system, want a brighter screen for outdoor use, and love the idea of professional-style camera control, the iPhone is a fantastic option.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Which of these giant phones is lighter to hold?
A: The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is lighter, weighing 214 grams compared to the iPhone 18 Pro Max at 249 grams.

Q: Does the iPhone 18 Pro Max support a stylus?
A: No, the iPhone 18 Pro Max does not support the Apple Pencil, but the Galaxy S26 Ultra comes with a built-in S Pen.

Q: What is a variable aperture on the new iPhone?
A: It is a physical part in the camera lens that opens and closes to control how much light enters, giving you professional control over your photos.
