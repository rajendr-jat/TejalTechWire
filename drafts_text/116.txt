TITLE: Apple iPhone 18 Pre-Orders Start While New Folding Phone Uses Clever Fake Bezel

CONTENT:
Apple is having a massive week. You can now pre-order the new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. These powerful new phones are available to order in 65 countries, including the US and the UK. But that is not all. Apple also announced its folding phone, the iPhone Duo, which features a unique design trick to help protect its screen.

A blogger recently discovered a hidden setting on the iPhone Duo called "Shift Left Edge". This option adds a black border, or a fake bezel, to the left side of the outside screen. A bezel is the border or frame that surrounds a digital screen. By adding this black border, the phone pushes your apps and text away from the folding edge so they do not get blocked by bulky phone cases.

HEADING: Why the Fake Bezel Matters

Phone cases often have thick edges to protect devices from accidental drops. On a folding phone like the iPhone Duo, these protective cases can cover up the outer edges of the screen. Apple solved this issue by letting users artificially shrink the active screen size with software. This clever compatibility feature ensures that your favorite heavy-duty case will not block your view or make it hard to tap your apps.

HEADING: Prices and Release Dates

The standard iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199 in the US and £1,199 in the UK. The larger iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at $1,299 in the US and £1,299 in the UK. Both of these models will ship to buyers starting September 18. Meanwhile, the folding iPhone Duo will cost $1,999 in the US and £1,999 in the UK. You can pre-order the folding Duo starting October 16, and it officially goes on sale on October 23.
