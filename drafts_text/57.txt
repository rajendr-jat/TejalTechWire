TITLE: iPhone Ultra Prices Leaked by Vodafone: Here Is the Expected Damage

CONTENT:
Apple is preparing to host a major launch event on Wednesday, September 9. At this event, the tech giant is expected to debut its very first foldable phone, rumored to be called the iPhone Ultra. It will also show off the new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. However, if you were hoping to buy one of these new models, you might want to start saving now. The upcoming iPhone prices leaked early on a Vodafone website, and they show a noticeable price jump across the board.

The leak suggests that pre-orders for the new phones will start just two days after the event, on Friday. While official prices will not be confirmed until the event, the leaked carrier information gives us a very good idea of what to expect. 

HEADING: Meet the Foldable iPhone Ultra

The biggest news from the leak is the price of Apple's first foldable phone, the iPhone Ultra. A Vodafone webpage in Egypt briefly posted the device before it was quickly taken down. When compared to current models, the foldable Ultra could cost about 65% more than the current top-tier iPhone. This means US shoppers can expect the base 256GB version of the iPhone Ultra to cost around $2,000, while UK buyers might see a price tag of £1,980.

HEADING: What the iPhone 18 Pro Will Cost

The regular iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are also getting more expensive. The leaked data suggests a price increase of about 10% for both models. This would bring the starting price of the iPhone 18 Pro to around $1,210 (or £1,210 in the UK), up from $1,100. The larger iPhone 18 Pro Max is expected to start at roughly $1,320 (or £1,320 in the UK). These price hikes are likely because the bill of materials—which is just the total cost of all the individual parts inside the phone—is estimated to be 40% higher this year.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When will the new iPhones be announced?

A: Apple is expected to announce the new phones at an official event on Wednesday, September 9, with pre-orders likely starting two days later on Friday.

Q: How much will the foldable iPhone Ultra cost?

A: Based on the leaks, the new foldable iPhone Ultra is expected to start at around $2,000 in the US and £1,980 in the UK.

Q: Why are the iPhone 18 Pro prices going up?

A: The prices are going up because the cost of the internal parts used to build the phone has reportedly increased by 40%.
