TITLE: Motorola Launches the Edge 70 Plus and a Crystal-Covered Razr

CONTENT:
Motorola just showed off its newest gadgets at the IFA 2026 tech show. The star of the show is the Motorola Edge 70 Plus, a sleek new smartphone that focuses heavily on beautiful design and long battery life. Alongside this phone, the company also revealed a special version of its Razr flip phone covered in shiny Swarovski crystals, plus a rugged smartwatch called the Watch Ultra.

If you love phones that look and feel great in your hand, this new lineup has a lot to offer. Motorola teamed up with color experts Pantone to create unique textures and shades for the Edge 70 Plus, making it stand out in a crowd of boring black plastic slabs.

HEADING: Stunning Design and Screen

The Motorola Edge 70 Plus is designed to be incredibly comfortable to hold. Both the front and back glass curve gently into the sides, so the phone feels smooth and slim. It features a bright 6.78-inch AMOLED screen. AMOLED is a screen technology that makes colors pop and blacks look incredibly dark. The display also has a 144Hz refresh rate, which means animations and scrolling look super smooth. You can get the phone in 4 unique colors, including a deep forest green and a soft blue. Each color option has a different texture on the back, giving some models a much grippier feel than others.

HEADING: Strong Battery and Surprising Camera

On the inside, the Edge 70 Plus is built to last. It comes with a massive 6,500mAh battery. Motorola says this giant battery can handle up to 34 hours of video streaming or 42 hours of phone calls on 1 charge. When you do run low, you can plug it in with 45W fast charging. For the cameras, Motorola did something unusual. The back of the phone looks like it has multiple cameras, but it actually only has 1 main 200MP camera and a tiny 2MP macro lens for close-up shots. There is also a 32MP selfie camera on the front.

HEADING: Smart Software and Tough Build

This phone runs the latest Android 16 software. It is fully loaded with Google's artificial intelligence tools, including Gemini and Circle to Search, which lets you look up anything on your screen just by drawing a circle around it. It also has a military-grade durability rating. This means the phone can survive extreme heat, cold, and high humidity, even though it is not a fully ruggedized phone. It also uses Gorilla Glass 7i to help protect the screen from scratches and accidental drops.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How long does the battery last on the Motorola Edge 70 Plus?

A: The phone has a huge 6,500mAh battery that can last for up to 42 hours of talk time or 34 hours of streaming video.

Q: What is the special version of the Razr phone?

A: Motorola revealed a special edition of the Razr 70 flip phone that is decorated with real Swarovski crystals.

Q: Does the Edge 70 Plus have good drop protection?

A: Yes, the phone uses Gorilla Glass 7i on the front and back, which offers up to 2 times the scratch resistance of standard glass and protects against drops.
