TITLE: iPhone 18 Pro vs Pixel 11 Pro: How these new flagships stack up

CONTENT:
If you are trying to decide on a new premium phone, you might wonder how these latest flagships stack up. The Apple iPhone 18 Pro and the Google Pixel 11 Pro represent the very best from 2 of the world's biggest tech companies. Instead of completely changing how their phones work, both brands have focused on making small, smart updates to their top devices.

These flagships, which is a word used for a company's most expensive and advanced phones, are packed with helpful features. They have incredibly bright screens, 3 cameras on the back, and plenty of built-in artificial intelligence to help you with daily tasks.

HEADING: Design and Weight Differences
Google changed the back of the Pixel 11 Pro by adding edge-to-edge glass to its signature camera strip, which helps the device lie flat on a table without wobbling. Apple kept its classic strong titanium and aluminum look for the iPhone 18 Pro, but added 2 new colors called Glacier and Burgundy. If you prefer a lighter device, Google's larger model, the Pixel 11 Pro XL, weighs 8 ounces, making it nearly 9 percent lighter than the heavy iPhone 18 Pro Max at 8.78 ounces.

HEADING: Beautiful and Rugged Screens
The standard models for both brands come with 6.3-inch screens, while the larger models are nearly 7 inches. Both devices use OLED technology, which is a special screen type that makes colors look highly vivid and blacks look very deep. They also feature 120Hz refresh rates, meaning the screen updates 120 times per second so scrolling looks incredibly smooth. To prevent damage from drops, Google added a new coating that makes the glass 2 times more resistant to scratches, while Apple used an anti-reflective glass coat to block glare from the sun.

HEADING: Staying with Your Favorite System
Despite these hardware upgrades, you probably do not need to switch from your current phone brand. If you already use a Mac laptop or an Apple Watch, the iPhone 18 Pro will work perfectly with the gadgets you already own. Likewise, if you rely on Google services, the Pixel 11 Pro is the logical choice. These updates are meant to satisfy existing fans rather than force anyone to learn a whole new operating system.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Is the Pixel 11 Pro XL lighter than the iPhone 18 Pro Max?
A: Yes, the Pixel 11 Pro XL weighs 8 ounces, which is almost 9 percent lighter than the iPhone 18 Pro Max at 8.78 ounces.

Q: What are the screen sizes for the standard models?
A: Both the standard iPhone 18 Pro and the standard Pixel 11 Pro have 6.3-inch screens.

Q: What new colors did Apple introduce for the iPhone 18 Pro?
A: Apple added 2 new color options called Glacier and Burgundy.
