TITLE: The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE Comes to India and Here is How Much It Costs

CONTENT:
Samsung is bringing its newest Fan Edition phone to a major new market. The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE comes to India very soon, giving shoppers a chance to get premium features without paying top-tier prices. If you have been waiting to upgrade your device, the wait is almost over.

The phone will go on sale in India on September 18. It costs INR 79,999 for the model with 8GB of memory and 256GB of storage. If you live in the US or UK, the phone starts at $699.99 or £699.00 for the version with 128GB of storage.

HEADING: Bright Screen and Fast Performance
The phone features a large 6.7-inch screen that is incredibly bright, making it easy to read even in direct sunlight. This display refreshes 120 times per second, which is a high refresh rate that makes scrolling through apps feel incredibly smooth. Inside, it runs on Samsung's own Exynos 2500 processor, which is the brain of the phone that handles your games and daily tasks with ease.

HEADING: Great Cameras and Long Battery Life
On the back, you get 3 cameras to capture different kinds of shots. The main 50-megapixel camera has optical image stabilization, which is a special technology that moves the lens to prevent blurry photos when your hands shake. There is also an 8-megapixel zoom lens and a 12-megapixel wide-angle lens. A large 4,900mAh battery keeps the phone running all day, and you can charge it up quickly with a 45-watt charger.

HEADING: Software Built to Last
A great thing about this phone is how long it will last. It comes with the new Android 17 software right out of the box. Samsung promises to support this phone with software and security updates for 7 years, meaning you will not need to buy a new device for a very long time.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When can you buy the Galaxy S26 FE in India?

A: The phone goes on sale in India starting on September 18.

Q: What colors does the phone come in?

A: You can buy it in 3 colors called Blueberry, Graphite, and Pistachio.

Q: Is the phone water resistant?

A: Yes, it has an IP68 rating, which means it is protected against dust and can survive being dropped in shallow water.
