TITLE: What You Missed In The New GTA 6 Extended Look

CONTENT:
Grand Theft Auto 6 is easily the most anticipated video game in the world right now. Netflix recently hosted a special 26-minute extended look of the game for 6 hours before it launched on YouTube. This new footage gives us a much deeper view of the fictional Vice City and its surrounding areas.

We just got a massive extended look at GTA 6, and there are several game-changing features you might have missed. From the release date of November 19 to how the main characters interact, this upcoming game is going to be huge. It could take players more than 80 hours to finish the main story.

HEADING: Playable Characters and Six Wanted Stars
The game features 2 main characters named Jason and Lucia. You can easily switch between them during high-speed police chases and normal gameplay. However, some missions will force you to play as only 1 of them. Players will also notice the return of the 6 Wanted Stars. This is a trouble rating system, and it means the police will chase you even harder than they did in the last game, which capped the system at 5 stars.

HEADING: A Brand New Reputation System
Rockstar Games is introducing a new Criminal Profile system. This is an honor tracker that changes based on how you behave in the game world. If you defend yourself against an attacker, the game will not judge you as harshly as if you start a random fight. You can even choose to talk your way out of trouble or calm down bad situations using dialogue choices on your screen. Friendly actions like feeding wild animals can also improve your reputation.

HEADING: Better Combat Controls
Players will also notice big updates to how weapon fights work. You can now quickly swap between 2 different guns without opening up a slow selection wheel. This menu normally pauses the game to let you pick a tool, so skipping it will make battles feel much faster and smoother.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When does GTA 6 come out?
A: The game is scheduled to be released on November 19.

Q: Can you play as more than one character?
A: Yes, you can swap between the 2 main characters, Jason and Lucia, during the game.

Q: What is the Criminal Profile system?
A: It is a system that tracks your choices and actions, changing how police and other characters react to you.
