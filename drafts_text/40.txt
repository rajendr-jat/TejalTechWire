TITLE: Pangram AI Detection Secures New $9 Million Funding To Spot Fake Content

CONTENT:
It is getting harder to tell what is real online. AI-generated text and photos are popping up everywhere, from job applications to online reviews. To help solve this problem, a startup called Pangram just secured $9 million in new funding for its advanced AI detection tools.

This fresh cash will help the company build better ways to spot computer-made content. The goal is not just to label things as real or fake, but to help people understand how much AI was actually used to create them.

HEADING: Spotting AI on Popular Platforms

Pangram is already putting its technology to work. The company recently teamed up with Substack, which is a popular online writing platform. Through this partnership, readers can see if their favorite newsletter writers are using AI to write their articles. Pangram has also released a brand-new tool specifically designed to catch AI-generated images.

HEADING: Why Checking for AI is Hard

The head of the company, Max Spero, explained that detecting AI is not a simple yes-or-no question. Sometimes people use AI just to fix their grammar, while other times a computer writes the entire piece. Pangram wants to show the difference between minor computer assistance and fully automated writing so users can make up their own minds.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much funding did Pangram receive?

A: Pangram raised $9 million to develop and expand its AI detection systems.

Q: How does Substack use Pangram's technology?

A: Substack uses the tool to show readers whether their favorite newsletter authors are using AI to write their content.
