TITLE: The iPhone 18 Lineup Is Ready to Order with Apple Foldable Coming Soon

CONTENT:
Apple has officially opened pre-orders for its brand-new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. If you are looking to upgrade, the new iPhone 18 lineup is finally ready to order, bringing faster speeds and better cameras to your pocket. While the standard, cheaper iPhone 18 model will not arrive until next year, tech fans can also look forward to Apple's very 1st folding phone, the iPhone Duo, which is coming soon.

These new Pro models run on Apple's 1st 2nm processor, called the A20 Pro chip. A 2nm processor is a super-tiny computer chip that runs much faster and uses less battery power than older designs. Along with better battery life, the phones feature a 48-megapixel main camera with a variable aperture, which means the lens physically opens and closes to let in different amounts of light for better photos.

HEADING: The Brand-New iPhone Duo Foldable
Apple is entering the folding phone market with the iPhone Duo, a wide book-style foldable. Pre-orders for this device will start in mid-October, with shipping starting 1 week later. It features a 7.6-inch inner screen when unfolded, support for a stylus pen, and a front camera hidden right under the screen. However, it is a bit thick at 11.3mm and heavy at 254 grams. This makes it thicker and heavier than its main rival, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, which is only 9.7mm thick and weighs 201 grams.

HEADING: Great Alternatives on the Market
If you want to save some money, there are several competitive options. Samsung's Galaxy S26 and S26 Ultra offer great screens and are cheaper than Apple's new Pro models. The S26 Ultra boasts a massive 200-megapixel main camera and a built-in stylus. Google also has the Pixel 11 Pro, which uses a new Tensor G6 chip that fixes past battery issues and includes magnetic wireless charging. For those who want a smaller folding phone, the Galaxy Z Flip8 is available at a similar price to Samsung's top traditional phones.

HEADING: Accessories Ready for the Launch
With new phones come new accessories, and brands like ESR are already prepared. They have launched a premium vegan leather case called the Atelier. This case features a special silicone leather that resists stains and dirt. It also includes a built-in metal stand that can rotate and is tested to last through 3,000 uses. The case comes with strong magnets that hold up to 1,400 grams, ensuring your wireless chargers and wallets stay securely attached. The Pro phone versions even offer 12-foot drop protection to keep your expensive new device safe.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When can I buy the new folding iPhone Duo?
A: Pre-orders for the iPhone Duo start in mid-October, and the phone goes on sale 1 week later.

Q: How much did Apple raise its older phone prices?
A: Apple increased the prices of the iPhone 16, iPhone 17, and iPhone Air models by $100.

Q: What makes the iPhone 18 Pro camera special?
A: It has a 48-megapixel main camera with a variable aperture, allowing the lens to adjust between f/1.5 and f/4.0 for better light control.
