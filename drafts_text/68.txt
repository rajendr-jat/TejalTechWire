TITLE: Google Pixel 11 Pro Review: What Google Still Gets Wrong

CONTENT:
Google released its new Pixel 11 phone lineup in 2026. Many users are looking at the Google Pixel 11 Pro review details to see if it is worth the high price tag. This small phone packs a lot of power, but it also highlights a frustrating issue. Google is withholding basic camera features from its standard, cheaper models.

In this article, we look at why this practice is raising eyebrows. We will also see how a much cheaper rival phone is beating Google at its own game.

HEADING: Small Size with Big Power

The Pixel 11 Pro is a rare find because it is a small phone with top-tier features. It has a 6.3-inch screen. This screen uses LTPO OLED technology, which means it can smoothly change how fast it refreshes to save battery life. Inside, you get a new Tensor G6 computer chip that makes the phone run fast and cool. Google also put larger physical sensors in the zoom and wide-angle cameras to capture more light. The battery is 4,850 mAh, which is a tiny bit smaller than last year. However, you now get faster 25W magnetic wireless charging, which lets chargers snap easily to the back of the phone. Sadly, wired charging is still stuck at a slow 30W speed, and you have to buy the charger brick separately.

HEADING: The Missing Camera Settings

If you buy the cheaper $900 standard Pixel 11, you might be disappointed. Google blocks this standard phone from using some basic camera tools. For example, you cannot take full-resolution 50-megapixel pictures. You also do not get a manual camera mode. A manual mode lets you control ISO, which adjusts how sensitive the camera is to light. It also lets you control shutter speed, which is how long the camera takes to snap the photo. Even a rival $500 budget phone, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max, includes these manual tools. Google is likely withholding these software features just to force people to buy the more expensive Pro models.

HEADING: Is It Worth the Price?

The Pixel 11 Pro is a premium device with a high price tag. The 256GB version costs $1,026.58 in the US and £1,034 in the UK. If you want 512GB of storage, the price is $1,181.84 or £1,199. The largest 1TB model costs $1,449 or £1,429. If you want a small phone that does not compromise on performance, the Pro is a great choice. But if you buy the standard model, prepare to miss out on some basic camera features.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Does the Pixel 11 Pro come with a charger in the box?

A: No, the box only contains the phone, user manuals, and a USB-C charging cable.

Q: What is the battery capacity of the Pixel 11 Pro?

A: The phone has a 4,850 mAh battery and supports 25W magnetic wireless charging.

Q: Can the standard Pixel 11 take 50-megapixel photos?

A: No, Google restricts full 50-megapixel photo resolution to the more expensive Pro models.
