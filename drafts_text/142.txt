TITLE: Snap Specs Are Here: We Tried the New $2,195 Standalone Smart Glasses

CONTENT:
Snap is releasing a brand-new pair of augmented reality (AR) glasses called Specs. AR means the glasses let you see digital images and menus floating in the real world around you. Unlike older smart glasses, these do not need a phone or computer to work. They are completely standalone devices.

We recently wore the new Snap specs to see how they perform. They cost $2,195 and are scheduled to launch sometime this autumn. While they are chunky, they offer an exciting look at the future of wearable technology.

HEADING: How They Look and Feel

The glasses are quite chunky and come in 2 sizes: 47 millimeters and 52 millimeters. At 132 grams, they are almost 2 times heavier than Meta's smart glasses. However, the weight is distributed well so they do not feel too heavy on your face. They fit snugly, and they will not slide down your nose even if you shake your head.

HEADING: Controlling the Magic

To control these glasses, you use your hands. Built-in cameras track your fingers, allowing you to pinch the air to click on menus or drag virtual items. For example, you can play a virtual game of dominoes with a friend on a real table. You can also hold up your palm to open the main menu or snap your fingers to take a quick photo.

HEADING: What's Next and the Cost

Snap plans to launch these glasses later in 2024. But with a price tag of $2,195, they are definitely not for everyone. The battery only lasts for about 4 hours on a single charge. For now, they are mostly built for developers and early tech fans who want to experience the future of the internet today.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much do the new Snap glasses cost?

A: They cost $2,195.

Q: Do they need to connect to a phone?

A: No, they are standalone glasses and do not need a phone or extra battery pack to function.

Q: How long does the battery last?

A: The battery lasts for up to 4 hours of use.
