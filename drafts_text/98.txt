TITLE: Meta Muse AI Agent Climbs the US App Store Charts After Launch

CONTENT:
Meta recently released a brand-new smartphone application called Muse. This new Meta Muse agent is designed to help you get things done automatically, like a digital personal assistant. In its first few days, the app quickly climbed to the number 2 spot on the Apple App Store charts in the US.

While hitting the number 2 spot sounds like a massive win, the launch is actually a bit slower than Meta's past releases. For example, when Meta launched Threads, millions of people downloaded it on the very first day. Muse has secured over 83,000 downloads on Apple devices so far, showing that people are interested, but they are not rushing in all at once.

HEADING: Understanding AI Agents

An AI agent is a smart computer program that does not just answer questions. It can actually take action on your behalf, like scheduling plans or organizing your digital life. Meta believes these helpful assistants are the future of technology, which is why they are investing heavily in Muse.

HEADING: Where to Find Muse

Right now, Muse is only available to users in the US. While it is doing very well on Apple iPhones, its Android version is off to a much slower start, ranking at number 338 in its category. You can also use Muse on the web and inside WhatsApp, but those numbers are not tracked in the app store charts.

HEADING: Tough Competition in the Market

Meta is not the only company trying to build these digital helpers. A rival app called Instinct is already making big waves in the tech world. Instinct is valued at $2.5 billion and lets users coordinate plans with friends through their AI helpers. It also connects with popular tools like 1Password and Stripe, making it a very strong competitor for Meta.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is the Meta Muse app?

A: It is a new artificial intelligence helper made by Meta that can perform online tasks on your behalf.

Q: How many people have downloaded Muse so far?

A: The app has been downloaded over 83,000 times on Apple devices in the US.

Q: Is Muse available outside of the US?

A: No, the Muse app is currently only available to users in the US.
