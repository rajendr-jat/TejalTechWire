TITLE: Poco X8 is official with a giant battery

CONTENT:
Phone maker Poco has just announced a brand-new smartphone for buyers around the world. The Poco X8 is official at last, and it comes with some very impressive features for a budget-friendly device. While most people were looking at the company's expensive flagship phones, this cheaper model might actually steal the show.

The biggest talking point is the phone's massive battery. It is designed to keep your phone running for days without needing a plug. Along with that, it has a fast processor, a bright screen, and excellent protection against water and dust.

HEADING: A Screen Built to Shine

The new phone features a large 6.83-inch screen. It uses AMOLED technology, which makes colors look incredibly bright and blacks look very deep. The screen also has a 120Hz refresh rate. This means the screen refreshes 120 times every second, making scrolling through apps look super smooth. Plus, the screen can get extremely bright, reaching up to 3,500 nits, which is a measurement of brightness. This means you will easily be able to see your screen even under direct, harsh sunlight. On the back of the phone, there is a 50MP main camera to take sharp photos, along with a 16MP front camera for selfies.

HEADING: Giant Battery and Reliable Power

Inside the phone, you will find a massive 8,340 mAh battery. A typical smartphone battery is usually around 5,000 mAh, so this capacity is much larger than average. It supports 67W fast charging, so you can fill it up quickly. It also has 22.5W reverse charging, which lets you use your phone like a power bank to charge up other gadgets with a cable. Poco says the battery is built to last. It can go through 1,600 full charges before the battery health drops below 80%. The phone is powered by a Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chip, which is the brain of the phone that keeps everything running.

HEADING: Price and Options

The phone will come in black, white, and orange. In Europe, buyers can choose between 2 main versions. The model with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage will cost €399.99, which is about $430. If you buy it early, you can get it for a discount price of €319.99, or about $345. The larger version has 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. It costs €449.99, which is about $485, with an early discount price of €359.99, or about $390.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What is the battery capacity of the new Poco phone?
A: It has a massive 8,340 mAh battery that supports fast 67W charging.

Q: Is the Poco X8 waterproof?
A: Yes, it has very high water and dust resistance ratings, including IP68 and IP69.

Q: What operating system does the phone run on?
A: The phone runs on the new Android 16 software with Xiaomi's HyperOS 3 on top.
