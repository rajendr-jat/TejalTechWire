TITLE: Automattic board forces CEO Matt Mullenweg to take sudden leave of absence

CONTENT:
The board of directors at Automattic, the giant company behind the popular website builder WordPress.com, has made a massive decision. The Automattic board forces founder and CEO Matt Mullenweg to take a sudden leave of absence from his leadership role. Mullenweg has run the company for years, but he was voted out of his daily duties against his wishes.

In a message to his staff, Mullenweg shared that the company's Chief Financial Officer, Mark Davies, worked with other board members to vote him out. Davies has now stepped in as the temporary boss, also known as the interim CEO. While Mullenweg is off duty at the main commercial company, he will still lead the free, open-source part of the project called WordPress.org.

HEADING: Years of Tension and Lawsuits

Mullenweg has been at the center of several major arguments lately. His company has been fighting a massive legal battle with a rival web hosting firm called WP Engine. Mullenweg accused them of using the WordPress name without paying for it, demanding they pay a large portion of their revenue. In 2024, the drama got so intense that Mullenweg told employees to quit if they did not agree with him, which led to 159 workers leaving the company.

HEADING: Mixed Feelings and Big Changes

The sudden shakeup has left many workers with mixed emotions. Some employees said they felt relieved or even happy about the change after years of stressful drama. However, others worry that losing their founder will create even more instability. This leadership change comes right after the company laid off 16% of its staff in April 2025.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Who is running Automattic now?

A: Mark Davies, the company's Chief Financial Officer, has taken over as the temporary CEO.

Q: Is WordPress still working?

A: Yes, the free and public part of the project, WordPress.org, is not affected by this decision and Mullenweg is still leading it.

Q: Why did the board force Mullenweg out?

A: The board did not give an official reason, but Mullenweg has recently been involved in major lawsuits, staff layoffs, and public arguments.
