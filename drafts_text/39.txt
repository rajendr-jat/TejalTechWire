TITLE: Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra Could Use Exynos Chip Again to Surprise Fans

CONTENT:
Samsung is making a major change for its upcoming flagship phone. Insiders claim the tech giant is building versions of the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra powered by its own Exynos 2700 processor. A processor is the main computer chip that acts as the brain of the phone. In recent years, Samsung reserved top-tier Snapdragon chips for its most expensive Ultra models worldwide. But this new leak suggests some regions will get the in-house Exynos chip instead.

This is a massive shift for smartphone fans. The last time an Ultra model used an Exynos chip was the Galaxy S22 Ultra back in 2022. That is a 5-generation gap. In the past, users in Europe and other regions have often felt jealous of the US, which usually gets the highly praised Snapdragon chips. This time, however, things might be different.

HEADING: Why Samsung is Making the Switch

Internal benchmarks show the new Exynos 2700 is incredibly powerful. Tests suggest it actually beats the upcoming Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip. According to leaks, the new Exynos chip offers 19% faster processing speeds than its Snapdragon rival. It is built on an advanced 2nm manufacturing process. The term nm stands for nanometer, which measures the size of the microscopic parts on a chip. Smaller numbers mean more parts can fit, making the chip faster and more efficient. This new technology promises a 26% reduction in power use and a 15% boost in overall speed.

HEADING: What This Means for Buyers

If these reports are true, the phones will be split by region. Buyers in the US will likely still get the Snapdragon-powered version of the Galaxy S27 Ultra. Meanwhile, buyers in Europe and South Korea will get the Exynos 2700 version. Samsung is also expected to launch a new model called the Galaxy S27 Pro alongside the standard S27 and S27 Plus, which will also share these chipsets.

HEADING: Other Big Design Changes

The new phone is expected to look a bit different from previous models. Leaked designs show a redesigned camera area on the back. Samsung plans to remove the older 10-megapixel camera with 3x zoom. Instead, it will rely on an upgraded 50-megapixel camera with 5x zoom. The phone will also feature a slightly larger battery to help it last longer on a single charge.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Which countries will get the Exynos version of the Galaxy S27 Ultra?

A: Insiders say that buyers in Europe and South Korea will get the Exynos 2700 version, while the US will get the Snapdragon variant.

Q: Is the Exynos 2700 chip better than the Snapdragon chip?

A: Internal tests suggest the Exynos 2700 is 19% faster than the Snapdragon 8 Gen 6, but real-world tests will show if it runs too hot.

Q: When will the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra be released?

A: The phone is expected to launch in early 2027, following Samsung's usual yearly release schedule.
