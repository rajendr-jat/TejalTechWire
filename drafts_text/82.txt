TITLE: Apple iPhone Ultra Launch Cost Could Be Higher Than Expected, New Report Says

CONTENT:
Apple is preparing for a massive launch event on September 9. Tech experts believe the company will finally reveal its very first foldable smartphone at this event. This highly anticipated new device is expected to be called the iPhone Ultra. 

We have been waiting a long time for a folding phone from Apple. A new report says the company has actually been working on this specific design for 10 years. However, if you want to buy this premium device, you might need to save up quite a bit of money.

HEADING: Why the Price Is Going Up
Apple originally wanted the new phone to start at $1,999. But a global shortage of computer memory chips and sudden price hikes have changed those plans. Because of these rising supply costs, the starting price is now expected to be $2,199. If you want the high-end version with 1TB of storage space, it could cost as much as $3,000.

HEADING: What the Phone Will Look Like
The phone will feature two screens of different sizes. When folded closed, it will have a 5.5-inch outer screen and look about the size of a passport. When you open it up, you will see a large 7.8-inch display. The fully unfolded screen will look similar in shape to Apple's Magic Trackpad accessory.

HEADING: Tough and Smooth Design
Many foldable phones have a noticeable crease where the screen bends in half. Apple has reportedly designed this phone to have an almost invisible crease. The hinge is made from strong metals like titanium and aluminum. It also uses special protective cover glass developed with Corning, the company famous for making durable phone glass.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When is the iPhone Ultra expected to be announced?

A: Apple is expected to announce the new foldable phone at its major event on September 9.

Q: How much will the iPhone Ultra cost?

A: The starting price is expected to be $2,199, and the most expensive model could cost up to $3,000.

Q: How big is the screen on the new foldable iPhone?

A: The phone has a 5.5-inch screen on the outside when closed, and it opens up to a large 7.8-inch main screen.
