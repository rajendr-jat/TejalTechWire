TITLE: Google Pixel 11 Pro Review: Excellent Cameras but a Weird Feature is Finally Dead

CONTENT:
The new Google Pixel 11 Pro has arrived, and it brings some major updates along with one surprising departure. If you are looking at Google's latest flagship phones, you might notice that a very unusual piece of hardware has vanished. Google has officially removed the infrared temperature sensor that first showed up on Pro models a few years ago.

In this Google Pixel 11 Pro review, we will look at how this new phone stacks up against the standard Pixel 11, what is replacing that quirky sensor, and whether the upgrade is worth your money. While some people laughed at the built-in thermometer when it first launched, many users grew to love it for checking everything from fevers to how hot the pavement was before walking their dogs. Now, that era is over.

HEADING: What is New and What is Gone

Instead of the temperature sensor, the Pixel 11 Pro features a new ring of lights around the camera flash called HiLight. This light glows in different colors to show you notifications or to let you know when the Gemini artificial intelligence assistant is listening to you. Inside the phone, the Pro model also shines with a brighter screen that uses special LTPO technology to change its refresh rate automatically, which saves battery power. You also get up to 16GB of RAM, which is short for Random Access Memory and helps the phone run multiple apps at the same time, though the basic Pixel 11 is still very fast in daily use.

HEADING: The Camera Upgrades

The camera is the biggest reason to choose the Pro version over the standard Pixel 11. Although both models share a 3-lens zoom system, the Pixel 11 Pro uses much better sensors for every single camera. It has a 50-megapixel main lens, a 48-megapixel telephoto lens for zooming, a 48-megapixel ultrawide lens for wide landscape shots, and a powerful 42-megapixel front camera for selfies. These high-resolution sensors work with Google's smart photo software to take incredibly detailed photos, especially in low light.

HEADING: Price and What is in the Box

You will have to pay a bit more for these extra features, as the Pro model costs about $200 more than the regular Pixel 11. Specifically, the version with 256GB of storage and 12GB of RAM starts at $1,098.82 in the US, which is £1,079.00 in the UK. If you want the top-tier 512GB version with 16GB of RAM, it will cost you $1,219.00 or £1,199.00. Keep in mind that Google only includes a USB-C cable in the box, so you will need to buy your own charger and a case, which now supports magnetic accessories.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Does the Pixel 11 Pro have a temperature sensor?

A: No, Google has completely removed the infrared thermometer from the Pixel 11 series and replaced it with a notification light ring called HiLight.

Q: How much does the Pixel 11 Pro cost?

A: The phone starts at $1,098.82 or £1,079.00 for the 256GB model, while the 512GB model costs $1,219.00 or £1,199.00.

Q: What accessories come in the box with the Pixel 11 Pro?

A: The phone only comes with a USB-C cable, so you must buy your own wall charger and protective case separately.
