TITLE: AI startup Nscale adds former OpenAI exec Fidji Simo to its board ahead of IPO

CONTENT:
A British artificial intelligence company called Nscale is making a major move. Nscale adds former OpenAI exec Fidji Simo to its board of directors. This big hire comes as the startup prepares for its upcoming initial public offering, or IPO, which is when a company lists its shares on the public stock market for the first time.

Nscale builds and runs massive AI data centers. These are giant warehouses packed with powerful computers that run heavy AI programs. The young company is growing incredibly fast. It is reportedly trying to raise up to $3.5 billion from investors to help fund its massive expansion plans before it goes public.

HEADING: Who Is Fidji Simo

Simo is a highly respected leader in the global tech industry. She recently served as the second-in-command at OpenAI, the famous creator of ChatGPT, before stepping down in July to focus on her health. Before that, she was the chief executive of the grocery delivery app Instacart, where she successfully guided the company through its own stock market launch in 2023. She also spent more than 10 years as a high-level executive at Meta, the parent company of Facebook.

HEADING: Why This Matters for Nscale

Nscale wants to use Simo's deep experience in growing tech companies to a massive scale. She joins an already star-studded board of directors that includes other big names like former Meta executive Sheryl Sandberg. Nscale's founder, Josh Payne, explained that Simo knows exactly what it takes to build computer systems that can handle billions of users. 

HEADING: What Comes Next for the Company

The startup plans to go public sometime this autumn. Since Nscale was founded only 2 years ago, its value has shot up because of the massive global demand for AI technology. The money they hope to raise will help them build more high-tech infrastructure to keep up with this booming market.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What does Nscale actually do?

A: Nscale designs, builds, and runs large computer warehouses called data centers that provide the heavy computing power needed to run modern artificial intelligence tools.

Q: When does Nscale plan to go public?

A: Nscale is planning its stock market launch for this autumn, though an exact date has not been officially announced yet.

Q: How much money is Nscale trying to raise?

A: Reports state that the company is aiming to raise up to $3.5 billion before it lists its shares on the stock market.
