TITLE: Travis Kalanick's Atoms Might Be Getting Into the Robotaxi Business

CONTENT:
Travis Kalanick, the co-founder of Uber, has a new startup called Atoms. The company recently raised a massive $1.7 billion in funding. Now, we are getting a clearer picture of what the company plans to do with all that money.

Reports show that Atoms is preparing to enter the self-driving car market. The startup wants to build technology for robotaxis, which are driverless passenger cabs. This is a big deal because Kalanick helped start the modern ride-hailing industry, and now he is trying to automate it.

HEADING: The Uber Connection

Atoms has already talked with Uber about using its driverless technology. Uber even invested $100 million in the startup. This makes sense because Uber already partners with several other self-driving car companies. Kalanick has described this new venture as unfinished business, suggesting he wants to complete his original vision for driverless rides.

HEADING: Hiring and Buying Spree

To make this happen, Atoms is planning to hire a lot of new workers. The startup is also looking to buy other companies. In fact, Atoms already bought a self-driving mining company called Pronto. Pronto is run by Anthony Levandowski, a well-known self-driving engineer who used to work with Kalanick at Uber.

HEADING: What This Means for Passengers

Robotaxis are still a rare sight in most cities, but the competition is heating up. With billions of dollars in funding, Atoms could speed up how quickly driverless cabs become common. While self-driving cars are not the only thing Atoms is working on, they are clearly a major part of the company's future.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much money has Atoms raised so far?

A: Atoms recently raised $1.7 billion in a funding round led by the venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz.

Q: Is Uber involved with Travis Kalanick's new startup?

A: Yes, Uber has invested $100 million in Atoms and has discussed using the startup's technology on its own ride-hailing network.

Q: What other companies has Atoms purchased?

A: Atoms bought Pronto, an autonomous mining startup led by former Uber self-driving head Anthony Levandowski.
