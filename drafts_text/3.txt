TITLE: Judge rules Pentagon ban on AI giant Anthropic is illegal

CONTENT:
A US federal judge rules that the Pentagon acted against the law when it banned the artificial intelligence company Anthropic. The court decided that labeling the tech firm as a security risk was illegal and had no actual basis. This ruling stops several government agencies from enforcing an executive order by President Donald Trump to block the company's software.

This is a major win for Anthropic, which makes the popular Claude AI tool. The judge made it clear that the government cannot use national security claims as an excuse to punish companies that criticize its policies.

HEADING: Why the Government Banned the AI

The disagreement started in February when government officials pushed Anthropic to remove safety guardrails from its AI systems. Anthropic's boss, Dario Amodei, refused to let the company's AI be used for mass spying or building robotic weapons. In response, President Trump ordered government agencies to stop using the software within 6 months, and the defense department labeled the startup as a supply chain risk.

HEADING: What Happens Next for Anthropic

Anthropic sued the White House in two separate courts to fight the ban. While this court decision is a big victory, the company still has to win its second lawsuit before the security risk label is fully removed. Even if Anthropic wins both cases, the Pentagon does not have to start buying its services again. However, the legal battle has not hurt the company's business, as its revenues recently jumped to $11.5 billion ahead of a planned public stock launch later in 2025.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Why did the US government ban Anthropic's AI?
A: The government tried to ban the company after Anthropic refused to let its AI be used for mass surveillance or autonomous weapons.

Q: What did the federal judge decide about the ban?
A: The judge ruled that the defense department's ban was illegal and baseless, and stopped federal agencies from enforcing it.

Q: Is Anthropic completely cleared to work with the government now?
A: Not yet, because Anthropic filed two lawsuits and still needs to win the second case to fully clear its name, though the Pentagon is not forced to buy its products.
