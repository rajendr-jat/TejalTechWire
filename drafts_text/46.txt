TITLE: Xiaomi Showcases Vibrant New 18 Fold Phone with Custom XRING Chip

CONTENT:
Tech fans have something exciting to look forward to. Phone maker Xiaomi just gave us an early look at its brand-new folding phone. Named the Xiaomi 18 Fold, this stylish device was showcased at the IFA 2026 trade show, just a week before its official launch. While onlookers could only view it behind a glass display case, the phone still managed to make a massive first impression.

The first thing everyone noticed was its striking, bright red color. It also features a unique, wider design that looks like a small passport. This shape makes it easier to hold and use than older, taller folding phones.

HEADING: A Comfortable and Unique Design

Xiaomi decided to give this phone very rounded corners. This choice should stop the device from digging into your hands during long chats or browsing sessions. Because of these curves, the smaller 5.38-inch outer screen looks a bit unusual, with sharp corners on the left and round ones on the right. When you unfold the device, you get a massive 7.58-inch main screen. Crucially, the crease in the middle of the screen is very hard to see, which is a major win for anyone who hates that bumpy line down their display.

HEADING: Cameras and Custom Power

For taking pictures, the phone has a selfie camera on each screen and a trio of lenses on the back. These rear cameras sit in a raised strip, which likely includes a main lens, a zoom lens, and a wide-angle lens. Under the hood, Xiaomi is using its very own processor, called the XRING O3 chip. A chip is basically the brain of the phone, and Xiaomi promises this custom-made brain will offer excellent speed and performance.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When will the Xiaomi 18 Fold be officially launched?
A: Xiaomi is scheduled to fully launch and detail the new folding phone next week.

Q: What size are the screens on this new phone?
A: The phone has a 5.38-inch outer cover screen and unfolds to reveal a larger 7.58-inch main display.

Q: What makes the design of this phone different from others?
A: It uses a shorter, wider shape with rounded corners to make it much more comfortable to hold.
