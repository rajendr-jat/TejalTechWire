TITLE: Google launches handy new updates to save your phone battery and edit slides

CONTENT:
Google is rolling out 2 big updates to make your daily tech life a lot easier. Google launches handy new software to help you figure out why your phone battery is dying so fast, and is also releasing a brand-new tool to help you design and tweak images inside your favorite work apps.

Both of these features rely on smart artificial intelligence (AI) to do the heavy lifting behind the scenes. Whether you are building a presentation for work or trying to make your smartphone last through the evening, these upgrades aim to save you time and frustration.

HEADING: Creating and Editing Images on the Go

The 1st update is a new service called Google Pics. This tool lets you create and edit images directly inside Google Docs and Google Slides starting today, with Google Drive getting the feature in the coming weeks. Instead of opening a separate design program, you can use AI to make precise edits or generate new graphics right where you are already working. For now, this tool is rolling out to people with Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra subscriptions, as well as most business accounts.

HEADING: Solving Your Pixel Battery Mysteries

The 2nd update is specifically for owners of the Pixel 10 and newer Google phones. It is a new feature called Battery Usage Summaries, which uses Gemini Nano AI built directly into your phone to explain your battery life in plain English. Instead of making you decipher complicated charts, your phone will show a simple message at the top of your settings page. It will tell you if your battery is draining faster than usual, such as if an app uses 15% of your power, and name the exact apps, like WhatsApp, that are hogging the most energy.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Which phones get the new battery summary feature?

A: The Battery Usage Summaries feature is rolling out to the Pixel 10 and any later phone models.

Q: Do I need to pay extra to use Google Pics?

A: Yes, Google Pics is currently limited to Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra subscribers, along with most Google Workspace business users.

Q: How does the new battery tool find power-hungry apps?

A: It uses built-in AI to monitor your background activity and compare your current daily power use against your normal average.
