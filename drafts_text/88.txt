TITLE: Apple Launches Surprisingly Cheaper AirPods and Lowers iPhone Trade-In Values

CONTENT:
Apple recently held its "Surprise and Shine" launch event, bringing some big updates for gadget fans. The tech giant introduced its brand-new AirPods 5, which are surprisingly cheaper and packed with better features than the older models. However, it is not all good news for your wallet, because Apple also updated its trade-in program and lowered the values for used iPhones.

If you want to buy the latest gear, these changes will affect how much you spend. While the new earbuds offer a better deal, you will get less money back if you plan to trade in your old phone to help pay for them.

HEADING: What is New With the AirPods 5

The new AirPods 5 come in 2 different versions. The basic model costs $129, and a more advanced model with a wireless charging case and touch controls costs $149. Both models now have active noise cancellation, which is a technology that blocks out background noise so you can hear your music more clearly. Apple says this noise cancellation is 50% stronger than before. The earbuds also feature a new shape designed to fit different ears more comfortably, and the battery lasts up to 5 hours on a charge with noise cancellation turned on.

HEADING: Changes to Trade-In Values

Along with the new earbuds, Apple changed how much money you get when you trade in your old devices. If you are trading in an iPhone, you will now get less money. For example, the trade-in value for an iPhone 16 Pro Max dropped from $720 down to $610. On the bright side, Apple actually increased the trade-in values for most iPads and Mac computers. A MacBook Pro can now get you up to $1,315, which is a big jump from the old limit of $855.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When can I buy the new AirPods 5?

A: You can pre-order the new AirPods 5 right now, and they will be available in stores starting September 18.

Q: How much did Apple lower the trade-in price for the iPhone 16 Pro Max?

A: Apple lowered the maximum trade-in value for the iPhone 16 Pro Max by $110, dropping it from $720 to $610.

Q: Do the new AirPods 5 support hands-free Siri commands?

A: Yes, you can use hands-free Siri commands, including nodding your head for "yes" or shaking it for "no" to respond to prompts.
