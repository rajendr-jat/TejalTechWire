TITLE: Hollywood Star Mark Wahlberg Is Coming to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 to Talk Business

CONTENT:
Movie star Mark Wahlberg is heading to the tech world. He will be a main speaker at the TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 conference in San Francisco. Instead of talking about his famous films, he wants to discuss business, investing, and how to build a successful brand. 

Wahlberg has spent years building a massive business career outside of Hollywood. He has started a production company, a fitness brand, and even a restaurant chain. Now, he is focusing on investing in startups, which are young companies working on new ideas. He wants to help these new creators succeed by sharing his own experiences.

HEADING: From Movies to Millions

Wahlberg will sit down for an onstage chat with Bruce K. Lee, a successful wealth manager who helps people handle their money. They will discuss how Wahlberg shifted from acting to professional investing. Wahlberg plans to share his personal mistakes, what he had to unlearn along the way, and how he uses his fame to get into major business meetings. He is currently very focused on supporting new tech companies in the healthcare and wellness fields.

HEADING: Event Details and Tickets

The big event takes place from October 13 to October 15 at Moscone West in San Francisco. More than 10,000 tech leaders and 250 speakers will gather at the conference to share ideas. If you want to go, you should buy your tickets soon. Ticket prices will go up after September 25, but you can save up to $200 by booking your spot early.
