TITLE: Apple iPhone Duo Launch Revealed Alongside Secret Samsung Deal

CONTENT:
Apple is making major waves in the smartphone world. Pre-orders for the powerful new iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max have finally started. But the biggest news is the upcoming iPhone Duo launch, which marks Apple's very first foldable phone.

To make this futuristic device possible, Apple had to partner with its biggest rival. A well-known leaker reports that Apple has signed a massive 3-year Samsung deal to supply the folding screens. This agreement will keep Samsung as the sole provider of these high-tech displays for the foreseeable future.

HEADING: A Costly Deal for Folding Screens
According to a social media leak, Apple will pay Samsung Display about $250 for every single foldable screen. This makes the display one of the most expensive individual parts inside the iPhone Duo. To help offset its environmental impact, Apple is using 35% recycled materials to build the new phone. This includes a battery made with 100% recycled cobalt and a hinge cover made with 100% recycled titanium.

HEADING: iPhone Duo vs Galaxy Z Fold8
The new iPhone Duo will go head-to-head with Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8. Both devices feature a 7.6-inch main inner screen when opened. However, Samsung's foldable is noticeably thinner and lighter than Apple's 254-gram device. Even so, Apple is bringing some unique features to the table. The iPhone Duo supports a stylus pen and has a hidden camera under the screen, which are 2 features Samsung recently dropped from its own foldable line.

HEADING: Flagship Price Drops and Upgrades
If you prefer traditional glass-slab phones, you have plenty of cheaper options. Google has released the Pixel 11 Pro series, which fixes past battery issues with its new Tensor G6 chip. Samsung is also dropping prices significantly. You can find large discounts on the Galaxy S26+ and the high-end Galaxy S26 Ultra. These Android phones are much cheaper than the premium iPhone 18 Pro models, though Apple's new A20 Pro chip still leads the pack in raw processing speed.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When can you buy the new iPhone Duo?

A: Pre-orders for the iPhone Duo start in the middle of October, and the phone will go on sale 1 week later.

Q: How much does Apple pay for the foldable screen?

A: Apple reportedly pays Samsung $250 for each folding display panel.

Q: Which phone has a faster processor between Apple and Google?

A: Apple's new A20 Pro chip in the iPhone 18 Pro series is much faster than the Tensor G6 chip inside the Google Pixel 11 Pro.
