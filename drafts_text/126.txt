TITLE: Apple unveils first foldable iPhone Duo and new game controllers

CONTENT:
Tech fans have a lot to talk about this week after a historic Apple launch event. Under new leadership, the tech giant finally did what many thought was years away: Apple unveils first foldable iPhone Duo to the public. The company also showed off the powerful new iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max models, which come with better cameras and faster processors.

On top of these major phone announcements, there are exciting developments for mobile gamers. Reports show that Apple is currently designing its own game controllers for iPhone. These handy gamepads might even carry the famous Beats brand name, making mobile gaming feel more like playing on a home console.

HEADING: The Foldable iPhone Duo and 18 Pro Models
The star of the show is the iPhone Duo, which is a wide folding phone that is about the size of a passport when closed. It features an anti-reflective inner screen with a barely visible crease, an under-display camera, and the return of the Touch ID fingerprint scanner. This high-tech foldable will cost $2,000 and starts shipping in October. If you prefer a traditional design, the new iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,200 and the larger Pro Max starts at $1,300. These Pro models feature a smaller Dynamic Island—the pill-shaped cutout at the top of the screen that shows notifications—and went on sale starting September 9, with shipping beginning September 18.

HEADING: New Game Controllers on the Way
For people who love playing games on their phones, Apple is working on some exciting new accessories. Computer code suggests the company is building two game controllers with traditional features like thumbsticks, direction pads, and motion sensors. While Apple already sells third-party controllers in its stores, these new ones might be Beats-branded devices. Using the Beats name could mean they will be made of lighter materials and sold at a lower price point than typical Apple-branded gear, while making it easier to play high-end games on your phone or Apple TV.

HEADING: Rivals in the Mobile Market
Apple is not the only company making waves. Other phone makers are preparing their own big launches. Honor is set to debut its Magic 9 series in China on September 28, while Vivo will show off its X500 series with a massive circular rear camera on September 21. Still, all eyes will likely stay on how Apple's new foldable compares to Samsung's latest folding phones.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: When does the iPhone Duo release and how much does it cost?
A: The folding iPhone Duo will start shipping in October and is priced at $2,000.

Q: What are the main features of the iPhone 18 Pro Max?
A: The iPhone 18 Pro Max features a massive 5,391mAh battery for the longest battery life in iPhone history, a smaller Dynamic Island, a variable aperture camera lens, and is eSIM-only, meaning it uses digital SIM cards instead of a physical plastic card.

Q: Will Apple make its own gaming controllers?
A: Yes, code indicates Apple is designing two of its own controllers, which may be released under the Beats brand name and feature thumbsticks and haptic vibration feedback.
