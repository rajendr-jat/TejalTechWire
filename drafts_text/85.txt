TITLE: Apple Surprise and Shine Launch Event: First Folding iPhone Revealed

CONTENT:
Apple is shaking things up with some massive news for smartphone fans. Today, the tech giant is hosting its major "Surprise and shine" launch event in Cupertino, California. This is the 1st product launch led by Apple's new CEO, John Ternus, and the star of the show is a brand-new folding phone called the iPhone Duo. The online stream for the event begins at 5 PM UTC.

At the same time, Apple is upgrading the way people shop. The company is getting ready for a big Saturday reopening of its Lakeside store in Essex, England, which now features a fresh design using sustainable materials. Whether you are looking for a futuristic new folding phone or a better in-store experience, Apple has a lot of updates to share.

HEADING: The New Folding iPhone Duo
The new folding iPhone is the main highlight of the launch event. Unlike previous models, this phone folds in half and features a special magnetic hinge. It runs on a powerful new A20 Pro chip, which acts as the main brain of the device, and supports the Apple Pencil. The phone will cost $2,000 for the base model with 256GB of storage, while the most advanced version will cost around $3,000. It also comes with 2 rear cameras with 48 megapixels each, and 1 front camera with 12 megapixels made for video calls. Alongside this folding phone, Apple is also showing off the iPhone 18 Pro, the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and new models of the Apple Watch.

HEADING: A Greener Store Experience
For shoppers in the UK, the Apple Lakeside store is reopening at 9 AM this Saturday after a major makeover. Apple used eco-friendly, sustainable materials to rebuild the store. The shop now features a special pickup area for online orders, a helpful Genius Bar for technical support, and free educational classes. Apple also added many accessibility improvements. These include tables and chairs at different heights, assistive listening systems for people with hearing difficulties, and more open space for wheelchair users. This is part of a larger plan, as Apple is currently updating 6 of its 39 UK stores.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: How much will the new folding iPhone cost?
A: The base model of the iPhone Duo with 256GB of storage will cost $2,000, while the high-end version will cost around $3,000.

Q: When does the Apple Lakeside store reopen?
A: The store will open its doors at 9 AM on Saturday with a brand-new design and improved accessibility features.

Q: Are there any other new Apple products coming out now?
A: Yes, Apple is introducing the iPhone 18 Pro, the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and new Apple Watch models, though the new AirPods 5 are delayed until 2027.
