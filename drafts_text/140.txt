TITLE: The Pocket-Sized Boox Palma 3 Launches With a Premium Aluminum Body

CONTENT:
Imagine a gadget the size of a smartphone, but with a screen that looks like real paper. That is the new Boox Palma 3, a pocket-sized ereader, or digital book reader. Boox just announced this tiny device, and it comes with some major upgrades. For the first time, it supports a stylus, which is a digital pen used for writing on screens.

Unlike older models that used plastic, the Boox Palma 3 features a tough aluminum body. It also runs on the Android 16 operating system. This means you can download regular apps from the Google Play Store, making it work much like a standard phone.

HEADING: Features and Battery Life
The device has a 6.13-inch E Ink screen, which is a display technology that mimics paper and is easy on your eyes. It includes 128GB of storage space, which you can expand up to 2TB with a memory card to hold thousands of books. It also has a 16-megapixel camera on the back to scan documents, and built-in microphones to record voice notes. The 3,950mAh battery can last for days of reading before you need to charge it.

HEADING: Price and Availability
The Boox Palma 3 will cost $320 when it goes on sale, which is $60 cheaper than the brand's Pro model. It will come in black or white. However, you will have to buy the compatible pen separately for $46. Boox has not given an exact release date yet, but the device is already listed on its official website.

HEADING: Larger Screen Options
If you want a bigger screen, Boox also announced 2 other color models. The Note Air6 C has a 10.3-inch color screen and costs $580, or $657 if you buy it with a physical keyboard cover. There is also the smaller 8.52-inch Note Mini C for $550, which is designed for working while you travel.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Can you write notes on the Boox Palma 3?
A: Yes, it supports a digital pen for writing and drawing, but you must buy the stylus separately for $46.

Q: How much does the Boox Palma 3 cost?
A: The device will cost $320 and will be sold in either black or white.

Q: Does the Boox Palma 3 have a color screen?
A: No, it has a black-and-white paper-like screen, but Boox did release 2 other models with color screens.
