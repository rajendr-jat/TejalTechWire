TITLE: Meta Debuts Muse AI Agent to Help You with Daily Tasks

CONTENT:
Meta is making a massive move to change how we use technology. The social media giant just released a new personal assistant called Muse. Tech experts call this tool an AI agent. An AI agent is a smart assistant that can do real tasks on your behalf, rather than just chat with you.

Meta debuts Muse AI agent to help everyday users save hours of time. You can use it to draft emails, plan vacations, and even buy items online. It works in the background even after you close the app. Meta is hoping this new tool will help it catch up with rivals like Google and OpenAI in the fast-moving AI race.

HEADING: How Muse Works and What It Does

Muse connects directly to your daily apps like calendars, emails, and shopping accounts. You can ask it to turn a social media video of a recipe into a grocery shopping list. You can also ask it to find and book a flight. For online shopping, Muse uses Stripe Link to pay securely so you do not have to worry about fraud. Meta plans to add support for other tools like 1Password and Shop Pay very soon.

HEADING: Price and Where to Get It

Muse is available right now in the US. You can use it on the web at muse.ai, download it on iOS and Android devices, or chat with it inside WhatsApp. It will also come to Meta's smart glasses in the near future. The tool is free for basic use, but you must enter a payment card to sign up. If you want to use it for heavy tasks, Meta offers two paid plans. These subscriptions cost $20 a month for the Power plan and $100 a month for the Maximum plan.

HEADING: Can You Trust It With Your Data?

To do its job, Muse needs access to your personal details, which has raised some privacy worries. Meta says it keeps your information safe by running the assistant on a private virtual computer in the cloud. Another AI security tool called Sentinel watches over Muse to stop it from sharing your passwords or making unapproved online moves. Users can also ask Muse to delete what it has learned about them, and they can opt out of letting Meta use their data to train its models.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: Is the Muse AI agent free to use?

A: Yes, Muse is free for basic use, but you must add a payment card to get started. Meta also offers paid plans starting at $20 a month if you want to use it heavily.

Q: Where can I access Muse?

A: You can use Muse on the web at muse.ai, download the mobile apps for iOS and Android, or message it directly inside WhatsApp.

Q: How does Meta protect my passwords and payments?

A: Muse runs on a private cloud computer and cannot see your actual passwords. A second security AI called Sentinel watches Muse to make sure it does not share data without your permission.
