TITLE: Nvidia Boss Jensen Huang Took A Live Call From Donald Trump On Stage

CONTENT:
Technology leader Jensen Huang took a live phone call from Donald Trump during a recent conference in Los Angeles. The head of the giant computer chip company Nvidia was speaking on stage when he was handed a folding smartphone to answer the surprise call.

The two men quickly started talking about artificial intelligence, which is often called AI. This conversation gave onlookers a rare look at how a major politician and a top tech boss view the fast-growing technology.

HEADING: Dismissing AI Fears

During the call, Trump told Huang that fears about the dangers of AI are a hoax. Trump argued that slowing down the development of AI would only help competitors in other countries like China. He compared the power of AI to the early days of the internet, saying the technology is simply too big to pause.

HEADING: Why the Conversation Matters

Huang seemed to agree with the positive message, pointing out how much the audience was cheering. For Nvidia, keeping AI development moving fast is very important for business. Nvidia makes the powerful computer chips that run AI systems, and its stock price has gone up by 33% over the past year. Downplaying the risks of AI helps keep their chip sales growing.

HEADING: Quick Questions Answered

Q: What phone did Jensen Huang use to take the call?

A: He used a Samsung Galaxy Fold 8 folding smartphone.

Q: What did Trump compare artificial intelligence to?

A: Trump said AI is even bigger than the internet and cannot be slowed down.

Q: How has Nvidia's stock performed recently?

A: Nvidia's stock has risen by 33% over the past year, though it did drop by a few percentage points on the day of the call.
