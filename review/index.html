Apple Next iPad Mini: What to Expect and the First Pixel 11 Settings You Need to Change

If you just bought the brand-new Google Pixel 11, you might notice it does not work perfectly right out of the box. Do not worry, because there are a few key Pixel settings you need to change to make your new phone run much better.

At the same time, exciting news is coming from Apple. The tech giant is almost ready to reveal its next iPad mini, and we already have a good idea of what to expect.

Tweaking Your Google Pixel 11 Settings

The new Google Pixel 11 has amazing features, but you must turn some of them on yourself. For example, you can use "Camera Looks" to pick your favorite photo style before you snap a picture. Social media creators can also turn on "Creator Suite" in the video settings for a helpful teleprompter that displays words on the screen while recording. To get faster power, you can plug in your phone when it is below 50% and turn on "Extreme Charging" in your battery settings. Finally, do not forget to turn on the "Battery Percentage" toggle so you always know exactly how much power you have left.

The Next iPad Mini is Coming Soon

Apple fans also have something major to look forward to, as a brand-new iPad mini is expected to hit stores by the end of October. This will be the first update to Apple's smallest tablet in 2 years. Rumors suggest the new device will feature a gorgeous OLED screen, which is a high-tech display that offers much brighter colors and deeper blacks. It should also have a faster A19 Pro or A20 Pro chip, better protection against water, and a new speaker system that uses vibrations. While the previous version launched with a $599 price tag, it remains to be seen if this upgraded model will cost even more.

Quick Questions Answered

When will the new iPad mini be released?

A: It is expected to go on sale by the end of October.

What is Extreme Charging on the Pixel 11?

A: This is a new feature that temporarily speeds up charging when your battery is below 50%, but you must turn it on manually each time.

How much will the next iPad mini cost?

A: Apple has not announced the official price yet, but the last model sold for $599, so the new one could cost the same or more.

How to adjust AirPods audio settings for a much better experience

Many people use their Apple earbuds straight out of the box without changing anything. But you do not have to stick with the basic factory setup. Learning how to adjust AirPods audio settings can make your music, podcasts, and phone calls sound much better.

You can customize these settings on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. It only takes a few taps to tweak how your earbuds work. This allows you to block out distracting traffic noise or hear people talking without taking your earbuds out of your ears.

Control What You Hear Around You

AirPods 4 and AirPods Pro have sensors on their stems. You can pinch these stems to switch between noise cancellation and transparency mode. Transparency mode lets outside noise in so you stay safe while walking on busy streets. You can also choose adaptive audio. This smart setting automatically changes how much background noise you hear depending on your current surroundings. You can change these options in the settings menu on your Apple device while your earbuds are connected.

Helpful Audio Tools

Your earbuds can do more than just play music. You can turn on conversation awareness, which lowers the volume automatically when you start speaking to someone. There is also a feature called personalized spatial audio. This creates a 3D sound experience that makes music feel like it is playing all around you. If you have mild hearing loss, you can even set up your earbuds to work as a hearing aid. This feature amplifies nearby voices after you take a quick, built-in hearing test.

Custom Equalizer Coming Soon

Right now, adjusting treble and bass directly is limited. However, Apple is launching a new tool called Custom EQ with the iOS 27 software update this fall. This tool is an equalizer, which is a built-in program that lets you manually change low, mid, and high sound frequencies. If you want to try it early, you can install the beta version of the iOS 27 software on your phone today.

Quick Questions Answered

How do I change settings on my AirPods?

A: Open the Settings app on your Apple device while wearing your earbuds or keeping their charging case open, then tap on your AirPods name.

Can I use AirPods as a hearing aid?

A: Yes, you can set them up to amplify voices by completing a built-in hearing test in your device settings.

When can I adjust the bass and treble on my AirPods?

A: You can do this when Apple releases the iOS 27 update this fall, or right now by downloading the beta version.

Elon Musk Finds a Faster Path to Build Gas Turbines for AI Power

Artificial intelligence uses a massive amount of electricity. Right now, there is a major shortage of power to run the giant data centers that train new AI models. To solve this problem, billionaire Elon Musk has started a secret project to build key parts for natural gas power turbines himself so his AI projects do not have to rely on the overloaded electrical grid.

The plan involves a secret metal-casting factory, called a foundry, built by SpaceX on 830 acres in Bastrop, Texas. Musk confirmed that making these turbine parts in-house will help get new natural gas plants online up to 18 months faster. This would give his AI company a huge advantage, but the move is already raising major concerns about air pollution.

The Power Grid Problem

Artificial intelligence requires specialized computer chips that need vast amounts of energy. The International Energy Agency expects global data center power use to double by 2030. Because the normal power grid cannot keep up, major tech companies are building their own private gas-fired power plants. However, the companies that make these massive gas turbines are completely sold out of production capacity until 2030.

Why Making Turbine Blades is Hard

The biggest bottleneck in making gas turbines is casting the spinning blades inside them. These blades must survive extreme heat up to 3,600 degrees Fahrenheit, which is actually hotter than the melting point of the metal itself. To handle this stress, each blade must be slowly grown as a single, perfect metal crystal inside a special vacuum furnace. Only 4 companies in the world can currently do this, and SpaceX is now trying to become the fifth.

The Pollution Problem

While this plan could speed up AI development, it comes with a dirty cost. Running more natural gas turbines means releasing more smog and harmful chemicals like formaldehyde into the air. In Memphis, Tennessee, where Musk operates his giant Colossus data center, local civil rights groups have already accused his company of running gas turbines without the proper environmental permits.

Quick Questions Answered

Why does Elon Musk want to build gas turbines?

He wants to generate private electricity for his AI data centers because the public power grid cannot supply enough energy fast enough.

Where is SpaceX building this new turbine factory?

The factory is located on an 830-acre site in Bastrop, Texas, near an existing Starlink facility.

What is the main environmental concern with this project?

Natural gas turbines release harmful air pollutants like formaldehyde and smog-forming chemicals, which can cause respiratory diseases.

Samsung Galaxy A35 and A56 Could Receive One UI 9 Beta Soon

Samsung is currently working on its next major software update for its phones. This update is called One UI 9, and it is based on Google's Android 17 operating system. While only a few of Samsung's most expensive phones have access to this new software right now, two of its popular mid-range phones might get a sneak peek very soon.

Tech experts have spotted test software for the Samsung Galaxy A35 and the Samsung Galaxy A56 on Samsung's private servers. This suggests that these two affordable models could receive the One UI 9 beta build before the final version rolls out. A beta is a test version of software that lets users try new features early and help find bugs before the official release.

Why This Is Great News for Budget Phone Owners

Samsung usually releases its test software only to its most expensive, top-tier phones first. Mid-range phone owners often have to wait many months for the final, stable version without getting to test it early. A stable version is the official, finished software that is safe for everyone to use. If the Galaxy A35 and Galaxy A56 get this test version, it means owners of these budget-friendly phones can try the new Android 17 features much earlier than usual.

What We Know About the Rollout

The evidence comes from Samsung's own test servers in Korea, where special software files for both phones were recently spotted. Samsung has also set up an official community forum section for the Galaxy A56 test program in India. Right now, only a few of Samsung's newest devices, like the Galaxy Z Fold8 and the Galaxy S26 FE, run the finished One UI 9 software because they launched with it. Meanwhile, the flagship Galaxy S26 series is currently on its 7th test version and should get the final update in the coming weeks, with older phones like the Galaxy S25 following shortly after.

Quick Questions Answered

What is One UI 9?

A: It is Samsung's upcoming software update for its smartphones, built on top of the Android 17 operating system.

Which budget Samsung phones might get the test update?

A: The Galaxy A35 and the Galaxy A56 are the two mid-range phones that have been spotted with test files on Samsung's servers.

What is the difference between a beta and a stable build?

A: A beta is an early test version of software that might have bugs, while a stable build is the final, finished version released to the public.

New WhatsApp scam alert feature arrives on Android as Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold gets major rivals

Big changes are coming to your mobile phone. WhatsApp is testing a clever new safety tool to stop scammers in their tracks. At the same time, phone shoppers are looking at exciting new alternatives to Google's latest expensive folding phone.

The new WhatsApp scam alert feature is now available to some Android beta users. A beta is a test version of an app before it is released to the general public. This tool uses smart technology on your phone to spot dangerous messages from unknown numbers. Meanwhile, if you do not want to spend $1,899 on the new Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold, other companies are launching impressive folding phones that might be better choices.

How the New WhatsApp Scam Tool Works

This safety feature uses on-device machine learning. This means a smart program on your phone analyzes messages without sending your private chats to WhatsApp or Meta. When you get a message from someone you do not know, your phone will warn you if it looks like a trick. You can then easily block the sender or mark them as safe. Because it is optional, you have to turn it on yourself in your account settings.

Great Alternatives to Google's Pricey Foldable

Google's new Pixel 11 Pro Fold is very thin, but it faces tough competition from other brands. For the same $1,899 price, the Motorola Razr Fold offers a massive 6,000mAh battery that charges much faster. It also has a better camera system and more storage. Another option is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8. This phone features a wider screen shape that is much better for watching widescreen movies without annoying black bars on the top and bottom.

Quick Questions Answered

How do I get the new WhatsApp scam feature?

A: It is currently only available to people using the WhatsApp beta test version 2.26.34.1 on Android phones, but it should roll out to everyone soon.

Does WhatsApp read my messages to find scams?

A: No, the computer model runs entirely on your physical device, so your private messages remain safely locked and encrypted.

What is a major drawback of the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold compared to its rivals?

A: It has a smaller 4,750mAh battery than last year's model, slower charging speeds, and basic 10.5MP and 10.8MP secondary cameras.
