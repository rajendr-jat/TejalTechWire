What You Missed In The New GTA 6 Extended Look

Grand Theft Auto 6 is easily the most anticipated video game in the world right now. Netflix recently hosted a special 26-minute extended look of the game for 6 hours before it launched on YouTube. This new footage gives us a much deeper view of the fictional Vice City and its surrounding areas.

We just got a massive extended look at GTA 6, and there are several game-changing features you might have missed. From the release date of November 19 to how the main characters interact, this upcoming game is going to be huge. It could take players more than 80 hours to finish the main story.

Playable Characters and Six Wanted Stars

The game features 2 main characters named Jason and Lucia. You can easily switch between them during high-speed police chases and normal gameplay. However, some missions will force you to play as only 1 of them. Players will also notice the return of the 6 Wanted Stars. This is a trouble rating system, and it means the police will chase you even harder than they did in the last game, which capped the system at 5 stars.

A Brand New Reputation System

Rockstar Games is introducing a new Criminal Profile system. This is an honor tracker that changes based on how you behave in the game world. If you defend yourself against an attacker, the game will not judge you as harshly as if you start a random fight. You can even choose to talk your way out of trouble or calm down bad situations using dialogue choices on your screen. Friendly actions like feeding wild animals can also improve your reputation.

Better Combat Controls

Players will also notice big updates to how weapon fights work. You can now quickly swap between 2 different guns without opening up a slow selection wheel. This menu normally pauses the game to let you pick a tool, so skipping it will make battles feel much faster and smoother.

Quick Questions Answered

When does GTA 6 come out?

The game is scheduled to be released on November 19.

Can you play as more than one character?

Yes, you can swap between the 2 main characters, Jason and Lucia, during the game.

What is the Criminal Profile system?

It is a system that tracks your choices and actions, changing how police and other characters react to you.

Samsung Galaxy S26 FE Review: What Is New In This Budget Phone

Samsung has just sent out its new phone for testers to look at, and we have our hands on it. The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE is the latest budget-friendly device in the popular Galaxy family, and it is ready for a full review. It offers high-end features without the massive price tag of the main flagship phones.

While this new model looks almost exactly like the older S25 FE, it brings some important updates under the hood. It has a faster processor and is packed with brand-new artificial intelligence (AI) tools to make your daily tasks easier.

What Has Changed

The biggest upgrade is the new processor chip, called the Exynos 2500. This chip is built using a tiny 3nm process, which is smaller and more efficient than the 4nm chip in the older model. In plain terms, a smaller nanometer size means the phone can pack more power into a tighter space. This new chip makes apps open faster, improves mobile gaming graphics by up to 33%, and boosts the brainpower of the phone's artificial intelligence by 40%. The phone also has a new camera design on the back that stands out slightly, instead of sitting completely flat.

New Smart Features

This is the first phone in this series to run Samsung's new software, called One UI 9. This software brings several clever AI tools. For example, a tool called Now Brief gives you personalized daily updates, while Now Nudge suggests helpful actions based on your notifications. The search tool can now look for multiple items in a single photo at the same time. There is also a feature called My FanCam that uses smart tracking to keep a moving person centered in your video clips automatically.

Price and What Is in the Box

The phone comes in 3 colors: Blueberry, Pistachio, and Graphite. You can choose between 128GB, 256GB, or 512GB of storage. Prices start at $700 in the US, £699 in the UK, and €699 in Europe. Just keep in mind that Samsung does not include a wall charger or a protective case in the box. You only get a USB-C cable, so you will need to use your old charger or buy a new one separately.

Quick Questions Answered

How much does the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE cost?

A: The phone starts at $700, £699, or €699 depending on your region.

Does the phone come with a charger?

A: No, the box only contains the phone and a USB-C cable, so you must buy a charger separately.

What colors can you get?

A: The phone is available in Blueberry, Pistachio, and Graphite.

Chinese automakers build humanoid robots to find their next big profit

Many Chinese automakers are now building humanoid robots because they think these machines will be their next big profit generator. They are following the lead of US electric car pioneer Tesla, which has been working on its own robot called Optimus. These car brands see a future where robots do hard physical labor in factories and homes.

To make this happen, companies are pouring massive amounts of cash into the technology. For example, the robotics branch of Chinese car company Xpeng recently raised over $900 million. This cash injection values the robot business at more than $6.3 billion. Other major car brands like BYD and Chery are also building their own mechanical helpers.

Why Car Companies Want Robots

Making cars is getting less profitable because of intense price competition. Humanoid robots, which look and move like people, offer a fresh way to make money. Car factories already have the heavy equipment and hardware experience needed to build physical robots. These companies believe that artificial intelligence, or AI, can now help these machines learn complex physical tasks very quickly.

When We Will See Them in Factories

We might see these robots at work sooner than you think. Hyundai plans to test humanoid robots made by Boston Dynamics in its Georgia factory this year. By 2028, the company expects these robots to handle tasks like sorting car parts. Meanwhile, Xpeng is preparing its own robot, named Iron, for real-world commercial work.

Quick Questions Answered

Which Chinese car brands are making robots?

Xpeng, BYD, and Chery are leading the way, while other brands like Changan and SAIC are also developing them.

How much money is being invested in these robots?

Xpeng alone recently raised over $900 million, while Mobileye bought a robot startup called Mentee Robotics for $900 million.

Can these robots think for themselves?

They use advanced AI systems, sometimes called embodied AI, which means the computer brain is built directly into the physical machine to help it learn how to move and work.
