Why AI is still giving us major real-world headaches

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 5d ago

Artificial intelligence is growing incredibly fast, but two new reports show that these smart tools are still causing some major real-world headaches. Whether you are walking down the street or chatting with a bot online, technology is tracking you. Unfortunately, it is not always getting things right, and that affects both your privacy and your patience.

First, a surveillance company called Flock Safety is facing a lot of anger over its smart security cameras. These cameras use AI to read car license plates and track vehicles. The boss of the company, Garrett Langley, recently said we need to find a compromise between safety and privacy. This came after reports showed police officers used the technology 46 times to stalk partners or ex-girlfriends. Politicians from both major parties are now trying to stop the government from buying this tech. While the company cut down how long they keep your data from 30 days to 7 days, critics say it is still too easy for people to bypass these rules.

At the same time, AI is failing to get things right in our private chats. A new study looked at 11,000 ads shown inside ChatGPT in 2026. It found that 33% of these ads had absolutely nothing to do with what the user was talking about. Even worse, 68% of the ads popped up when the user was just trying to learn or study, with no plans to buy anything. While OpenAI tries to match ads to the words you type, the system still makes a lot of mistakes and shows you things you do not want.

These issues show that AI still has a long way to go. Right now, it is either watching us too closely on our streets, or failing to understand us when we are online. We can only hope these systems get a lot better at respecting our space and our time.

Original reporting by TejalTechWire, based on multiple industry sources.
