From Phone Privacy to Heavy-Duty Motors: Big Tech Changes in 2026

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 7d ago

Tech is getting some big upgrades in 2026, from new privacy settings on your phone to a giant motor on the highway. Whether you want to protect your personal information or you just love powerful machines, these updates show how technology is changing both our digital and physical worlds.

First, Instagram is rolling out a new setting in 2026 to help you control your data. It is called "Activity from other businesses." This tool stops the app from using what you do on other websites to target you with ads or train its AI, which is computer software that mimics human thinking. To turn this off, go to your Instagram profile, open the Accounts Center, and look for this setting. iPhone and Android users can also go into their phone settings to block the app from tracking them outside of Instagram. This will not stop ads entirely, but it stops other websites from sharing your shopping habits with Meta.

On the physical side of tech, Honda is making news with its biggest motorcycle engine for 2026. While Honda is famous for small scooters, its largest motor is a massive 1833cc engine inside the Goldwing motorcycle. In simple terms, "cc" stands for cubic centimeters, which measures how much space is inside the engine for fuel and air. This engine is actually bigger than the motors in many small cars. It makes 125 horsepower, which is 10 more horsepower than a 2001 Honda Civic.

This giant engine is built for long, smooth road trips. The motorcycle weighs 800 pounds, so it needs a lot of strength to move. Because the engine is so large, it does not have to spin very fast to do its job, topping out at 5500 rpm, which stands for rotations per minute. This keeps the ride quiet and smooth, and riders even report getting up to 50 miles per gallon.

Whether you are locking down your personal data or dreaming of a long highway cruise, 2026 has some great tech to offer. It is always smart to stay in the loop so you can get the most out of your gadgets and machines.
