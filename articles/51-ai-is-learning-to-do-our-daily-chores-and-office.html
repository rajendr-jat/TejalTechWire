AI is Learning to Do Our Daily Chores and Office Work

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 6d ago

Artificial intelligence is moving beyond simple chatting to actually doing real work for us, both on our computers and in the physical world. Tech giant OpenAI is rolling out new tools to handle complex office tasks, while a robotics startup has built a brain that lets robots learn physical chores just by watching a short video. Together, these developments show how AI is becoming a practical helper in our daily lives.

On the digital side, OpenAI is pushing what they call "AI agents" through a new service called ChatGPT Work, included in their $20 monthly subscription. AI agents are smart software assistants that can perform entire, multi-step tasks on their own rather than just answering questions or writing text. The company wants to make this powerful tech incredibly easy to use. Instead of typing complicated commands, you can simply speak to the system naturally, like talking to a friend, and let the AI do the heavy lifting behind the scenes.

Meanwhile, robots are getting a similar upgrade in the physical world. A company called Generalist AI recently shared details about its new robot brain, called GEN-1.5. This is a "foundation model," which is a large AI program trained on massive amounts of data. Using a trick called "physical prompting," you can show the robot a video demonstration of a physical task lasting only 3 to 12 seconds. The robot can then copy the action immediately without any extra programming. It can successfully copy physical tasks 59% of the time on its first try, and that number jumps to 83% after just 5 minutes of practice. It can even watch a human hand do something and copy it with its own robotic hand.

Why does this matter to you? It means we are getting closer to a world where you do not need to know how to code to use advanced technology. Whether you want a digital assistant to organize your business files or a physical robot to help sweep the floor, you will soon be able to just show or tell them what you need. AI is quickly transforming from a fun novelty into a genuinely useful tool for everyone.
