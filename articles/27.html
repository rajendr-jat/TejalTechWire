Smartwatch updates are ending soon plus how to choose your next flashlight

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 1d ago

Big changes are coming to your everyday gear, from the smartwatches on your wrist to the emergency lights in your home.

To start, several popular smartwatches will stop getting software updates in 2026. This includes the Apple Watch Series 8, the Google Pixel Watch 2, and the Samsung Galaxy Watch 5. Google only promises 3 years of support for its watches, which is much shorter than its phones. While these older watches will still turn on, they will no longer get security patches. Security patches are digital safety updates that block hackers from stealing your personal details. If you use your watch to pay for items or track your health, an outdated device could put your data at risk. This matters to you because wearing an unsupported device can compromise your online safety.

Also, if you want to replace your old home gear, you should know how to buy the right flashlight. Many people only look at brightness, but the beam pattern is just as important. A beam pattern is the shape and spread of the light that shines out. You can choose between a flood light and a throw light. A flood flashlight spreads a soft, wide bubble of light over a large space nearby. It is the best choice for walking your dog at night or working in a dark garage.

In contrast, a throw flashlight casts a highly focused, tight beam across a very long distance. This makes it a great tool for search teams or boaters who need to see far ahead in the dark, though it does not let you see your sides. Choosing the correct light pattern will save your battery and keep you safe.

Keeping your wearable tech secure and choosing the right tools for your home are easy ways to stay prepared. Taking a quick look at your gadgets today can save you from a major headache tomorrow.
