Tesla is asking people if they want to buy Cybercab fleets

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 21h ago

Tesla is asking people if they want to run Cybercab fleets to help build its brand-new self-driving taxi network. The electric car company recently posted an online form for businesses that are interested in buying these high-tech vehicles or setting up the stations needed to support them.

This is a major shift in strategy for the company. In the past, Tesla seemed to want to run its entire robotaxi service on its own. Now, by inviting outside businesses to participate, the company could get its self-driving cars onto public roads much faster.

What Tesla Is Planning

The new sign-up form asks interested businesses how they would like to participate. Some companies might want to buy a whole group of Cybercabs to run their own local taxi services. Others might want to build charging stations or parking hubs where these robotic cars can rest and get cleaned. By letting outside partners handle these daily chores, Tesla can focus on building the cars and the software.

A Change in Strategy

For many years, Tesla boss Elon Musk said that regular car owners would make money by renting out their own personal cars as self-driving taxis. He even predicted this would happen by 2020, but that plan never worked out. Instead, Tesla designed the Cybercab, which is a special car built only to be a taxi. It does not even have a steering wheel or gas pedals.

Why This Matters

Managing a massive fleet of taxis is incredibly hard work that requires lots of space and staff. Other autonomous vehicle companies, like Waymo, already use outside partners to clean, charge, and park their cars. By opening its doors to these helper companies, Tesla can launch its taxi service in many different cities without having to spend billions of dollars on its own parking lots.

Quick Questions Answered

Can regular people buy a Cybercab right now?

A: No, the new form is designed for businesses that want to buy large groups of vehicles or build charging stations.

What is a robotaxi?

A: It is a fully self-driving car that carries passengers to their destinations without a human driver behind the wheel.

When will these self-driving taxi fleets start operating?

A: Tesla has not announced an official launch date yet, as the company still needs to get approval from government road safety regulators.
