The Pentagon releases custom ChatGPT version and Grok AI for military staff

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 17h ago

The US military is getting its own high-tech digital assistants. The Pentagon releases a custom ChatGPT version alongside Elon Musk's Grok AI to help millions of military and civilian workers do their jobs much faster. These new tools are highly secure and designed specifically to handle sensitive government information without leaking it to the public.

These tools are hosted on a special, private website portal called GenAI.mil. This secure system already offered Google Gemini to workers. By launching these custom programs, the US Department of Defense wants to give its staff the power of modern artificial intelligence while keeping national secrets safe.

How Military Staff Will Use the AI

The new tools will help with a lot of everyday, tedious paperwork. ChatGPT Mil will focus on routine office tasks like writing emails, organizing files, planning logistics, and analyzing policy. On the other hand, the military version of Grok will help with more complex duties. This includes tracking supply chains and helping operators analyze market research to make fast decisions during active missions.

Keeping Government Secrets Safe

Normally, when you use public AI tools, the companies behind them can collect your data to train their models. The military's new portal blocks this data collection entirely. This means 3 million military and civilian personnel can upload documents and ask questions without worrying about defense secrets ending up on the public internet. Already, more than 1.7 million workers have signed up to use the portal.

Why One Major AI is Missing

While Google, OpenAI, and xAI are on board, one major AI company is missing from the Pentagon's portal. Anthropic, the creator of the popular Claude chatbot, is not included. The US government labeled Anthropic a supply-chain risk after the company refused to give the military unrestricted use of its tools. Anthropic wanted strict safety rules on how the military could use its AI, which led to a legal fight between the startup and the government.

Quick Questions Answered

Who is allowed to use these military AI tools?

The tools are available to about 3 million Department of Defense employees, including both active military personnel and civilian workers.

Are these military AI tools the same as the ones the public uses?

They work similarly, but they are highly secured so that sensitive government data cannot be saved or tracked by outside tech companies.

Which AI models are currently available on the military portal?

Personnel can currently use customized versions of OpenAI's ChatGPT, xAI's Grok, and Google's Gemini.
