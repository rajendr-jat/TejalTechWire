AI is facing a reality check on Wall Street, but the actual tech is getting better

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 3d ago

The artificial intelligence world is seeing a major split right now, with big-money investments facing government trouble while regular tech tools get smarter and cheaper.

First, look at the financial side of AI. A famous AI-focused investment company called Situational Awareness is currently under investigation by US regulators. The Securities and Exchange Commission, or SEC, is asking banks for records about the fund. This comes after a massive drop in AI stock values in July 2024 erased billions of dollars. The fund was started by a former OpenAI employee and grew incredibly fast before the crash. Now, it is serving as a warning that the giant wave of AI money might be slowing down.

But while the big money is hitting a bump, the actual technology is still moving forward. A company called Fastino just released a new free AI tool called GLiNER2.5. This tool helps computers pull specific details out of huge documents, like finding names in contracts or medical records. Normally, this task is either very expensive or requires giant computer chips. The new tool can read documents up to 4,096 words long. It uses a clever shortcut that measures where words start and end instead of scanning every single option. This means even a two-person company can use it on basic office computers without spending a fortune.

Why does this matter to you? It shows that the future of AI is not just about giant, expensive systems that cost millions of dollars to run. Instead, smaller and more efficient tools are making it easier for regular businesses to protect your private data, read contracts, and handle customer support. While Wall Street is worrying about a potential AI bubble bursting, software developers are quietly making tools that are actually useful for everyday life.

It turns out that the real value of AI might not be in the stock market, but in these quiet, practical upgrades.
