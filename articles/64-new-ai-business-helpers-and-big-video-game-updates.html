New AI Business Helpers and Big Video Game Updates

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 7d ago

This week, a smart AI startup raised millions of dollars to help small businesses grow, while a major gaming studio finally spoke out about massive leaks of its next big video game. Whether you run a side hustle or just love playing games, these updates show how fast technology is changing our daily lives.

To start with, a startup called Runable just secured $21 million in new funding. Runable makes an AI agent, which is a smart software helper that can perform online tasks for you. While many AI tools just write text or build simple websites, Runable wants to help companies actually find customers. It can set up online ads, post on social media, and improve search rankings on Google. The company was started in 2025 and already has 1.7 million users, with many living in the US and the UK.

Meanwhile, the creators of Grand Theft Auto 6 are dealing with a different kind of tech challenge. Rockstar Games released a statement about recent, unauthorized videos of their upcoming game being shared online. They called the leaks deeply upsetting for their staff, especially since they wanted to show off the finished product themselves. The leaks were shared by a group trying to promote a cryptocurrency, which is a form of digital money. Fortunately, the game is still on track. Fans can watch a special preview on Netflix at 3 PM Eastern Time, and the game will officially launch on November 19.

It is clear that tech is bringing both great new tools and big security headaches. We will keep an eye on how these stories unfold.
