Earbuds for Humans and Sprinting Robots Break New Running Records

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 3d ago

We have some exciting news from the running world, featuring brand-new lightweight earbuds for humans and incredibly fast robots that do not know how to stop.

First, let us look at the track, where human athletic records are falling to machines. At the World Humanoid Robot Games, a special competition for robots built with 2 legs, a machine called Tiangong Ultra ran 100 meters in just 9.39 seconds. That is faster than Usain Bolt's famous world record of 9.58 seconds. Another robot called Lightning was close behind at 9.47 seconds. While these machines are incredibly fast, they still have 1 major problem. They do not know how to slow down, so they have to crash into soft pads at the finish line and get carried away on stretchers.

If you prefer to do your own running, you might want some new gear to help you along. Shokz has just released 2 new pairs of lightweight headphones for $130 each. The first is called the OpenFit Air 2. These earbuds weigh only 7.2g, which is about the same as a few paperclips. They use an open-ear design. This means they do not seal your ear canal, allowing you to hear traffic and stay safe in busy cities. They also feature special software to give you deeper bass and a richer sound.

The second model is called the OpenRun Air 2. These wrap around your head and use bone conduction. This is a smart technology that sends music vibrations through your cheekbones instead of directly into your ears. Both pairs of earbuds have great battery life. The OpenFit Air 2 lasts for 8 hours on a single charge, while the OpenRun Air 2 lasts for 10 hours.

This matters to you because technology is changing how we move, exercise, and stay safe. Whether you want a better way to listen to music on your daily jog or want to marvel at the future of robotics, technology is sprinting forward. Just remember to watch where you are going so you do not crash like the robots.
