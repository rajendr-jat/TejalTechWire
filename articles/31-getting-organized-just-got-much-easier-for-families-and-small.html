Getting Organized Just Got Much Easier for Families and Small Businesses

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 11d ago

New smart tools are hitting the market to help busy families and small business owners organize their chaotic daily schedules. These new releases show how artificial intelligence and smart screens are finally becoming affordable and simple enough for everyday use. Managing a hectic household or running a small company can feel like a constant struggle, but these new tools aim to do the heavy lifting for you.

For busy homes, a company called Linkdaze has launched a smart touchscreen display designed to keep everyone on track. This digital screen comes in 10.1-inch and 15.6-inch sizes and hangs on your wall. It brings together separate schedules from Google, iCloud, and Outlook into one color-coded view. You can use it to track chores, display family photos, and even plan meals. In fact, you can snap a photo of a paper recipe, and its built-in AI will automatically create a digital meal plan and a shopping list. The smaller model costs $119.99, and unlike competitors that charge $79 a year, it does not require a monthly subscription.

For independent business owners, a company called HoneyBook is releasing a tool that links business records directly to Claude, a popular AI assistant. An AI assistant is a smart computer program that can answer questions and perform digital tasks for you. Big corporations with over $5 billion in revenue use AI tools every day, but only 29% of businesses making under $100 million do. This new connection helps smaller businesses close that gap. Owners can simply ask the AI helper if a client has paid an invoice or where a new lead stands, and get an immediate answer. It can also draft contracts, send out invoice reminders, and help manage customer interactions.

Using these smart tools can save hours of tedious work every week. Studies even show that small businesses adopting AI earn nearly 5 times more revenue than those that do not. Whether you are dividing up household chores or chasing unpaid business bills, letting technology handle the boring details is a great way to win back your free time.
