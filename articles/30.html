How to Master Your Modern Car Tech and Avoid Hidden Costs

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 2d ago

Modern cars are packed with clever technology, but they can also bring unexpected stress and extra costs to your daily drive. Whether you are dealing with noisy screen software on your dashboard or shopping for a new vehicle, it helps to know what you are getting into. Taking control of your car's settings and understanding new engine types can make your travels much safer and save you money.

If you use Apple CarPlay, which displays your phone apps on your car screen, you might find Siri very annoying. The digital voice assistant often interrupts your music or podcasts to read out text messages. To fix this, you can turn on the Driving Focus setting on your phone, which acts like a "do not disturb" mode while you drive. You can also turn off the setting that tells Siri to read notifications aloud, and increase the pause time so Siri does not stop listening to you when road noise gets too loud.

While software can be adjusted for free, choosing the right physical car is a much bigger financial decision. Hybrid cars, which use both a regular gas engine and an electric motor with a battery, are highly popular for saving fuel. However, they are much heavier than standard cars because of their large batteries. They also cost thousands of dollars more to buy.

For example, a 2026 Toyota Prius has a starting price of $29,745, which is more than $6,000 extra compared to a standard gas-powered Toyota Corolla at $23,125. Similarly, choosing a hybrid option on a truck like the 2026 Ford F-150 can add $6,120 to the final bill. While these vehicles save you money on gas over time, the high initial price and heavy weight are serious factors to think about before you visit a dealership. Keeping your dashboard quiet and choosing the right engine will help you enjoy a peaceful journey.
