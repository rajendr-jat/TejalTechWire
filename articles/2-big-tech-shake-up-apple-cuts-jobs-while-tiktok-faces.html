Big Tech Shake-Up: Apple Cuts Jobs While TikTok Faces Huge Fine

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 12d ago

Big changes are hitting the tech world as Apple cuts jobs to focus on new artificial intelligence, and TikTok agrees to pay a massive $400 million fine over child privacy. These updates show how major tech companies are shifting their priorities to deal with new laws and changing technology. For everyday users, this means your favorite apps might get safer, and the gadgets you buy in the future will look very different.

Apple is laying off about 200 workers. Around 100 of these jobs are in the Vision Pro headset division, especially in gaming and immersive video, which is a high-tech video format that makes you feel like you are inside the scene. Another 100 cuts are coming from the Siri team. Apple is moving away from its old voice assistant to focus on generative AI, which is smart software that can write text and answer complex questions like a human. The Vision Pro headset costs $3,699, which is too expensive for most people. Because of this, Apple is shifting its attention to lighter smart glasses and might not release a new headset until 2028.

Meanwhile, TikTok is dealing with a major legal battle. The company agreed to pay $400 million to settle a lawsuit with the US government. Officials accused TikTok of violating child privacy laws by collecting data from children without their parents' permission. TikTok also allegedly refused to delete accounts when parents asked them to. Under the new agreement, TikTok will pay $300 million immediately and another $100 million later. The app has also upgraded its safety controls to give parents more power over what their kids see.

Both of these stories show that the tech landscape is constantly moving. Whether companies are adjusting to what buyers want or trying to follow the rules, the gadgets and apps we use every day are always evolving.
