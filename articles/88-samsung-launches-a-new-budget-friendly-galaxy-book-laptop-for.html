Samsung Launches a New Budget-Friendly Galaxy Book Laptop for $799

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 23h ago

Samsung just announced a more affordable option for its latest laptop family. The brand new Samsung Galaxy Book budget laptop starts at only $799, making it a much cheaper alternative to its premium siblings that usually cost well over $1,000.

This new portable computer is designed to go head-to-head with other affordable laptops on the market. It offers a balance of solid performance, long battery life, and helpful smart features without the heavy price tag.

What You Get for the Price

This sleek 14-inch laptop features a screen that reduces glare, which is great for working in bright rooms. Inside, you can choose between Intel Core 3 or Core 5 processors, which are the main chips that run the computer. You can also customize the machine with up to 16GB of memory, which helps the laptop run multiple apps at once, and up to 1TB of storage space for all your files.

Battery Life and Smart Features

One of the best features is the battery, which Samsung says can last up to 25 hours on a single charge. If you run out of power, the laptop supports fast charging to get you back to work quickly. It also comes with built-in artificial intelligence, or AI, which can help you summarize notes and quickly grab text from your screen.

Quick Questions Answered

How much does the new Samsung Galaxy Book cost?

A: The price starts at $799, but it can go up depending on the storage and memory you choose.

What colors is this laptop available in?

A: It is available in Gray and a new color option called Violet Silver.

Does it have ports to plug in accessories?

A: Yes, it has two USB Type-C ports, one standard USB Type-A port, an HDMI port, and a headphone jack.
