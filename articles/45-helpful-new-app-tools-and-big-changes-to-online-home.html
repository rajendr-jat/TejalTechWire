Helpful New App Tools and Big Changes to Online Home Searching

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 5d ago

Two big tech companies are making major changes this week to how you scroll through social media and search for rentals online. First, the popular social media app Threads is testing a handy new feature that automatically turns podcast audio into text. This process is called transcription, which simply means the app writes down the spoken words so you can read them on your screen. This tool will let you enjoy audio clips on your feed even when you have your phone on silent.

Why does this matter to you? If you are on a busy train or in a quiet office, you can still follow along with your favorite shows without needing your headphones. Meta, the company that owns Threads, wants to attract more audio creators to its platform. Since the app already reached a massive 500 million monthly users in June, adding these helpful tools makes the app much more useful for everyday people.

Meanwhile, there is big news if you are looking for a new place to live. The Federal Trade Commission, which is the US government agency that protects consumers, has settled a major legal case with the popular property websites Zillow and Redfin. The government accused the two companies of violating antitrust laws. These are rules that stop giant companies from making deals that block competition and lead to higher prices.

The government argued that a 2025 partnership between the two websites cut down on rental options, which made prices go up. To settle the case, Redfin must now restart its own rental advertising business to compete fairly with Zillow. This is a big win for renters and landlords because more website competition usually means more rental choices and better rental prices when you search for a home online.

Whether you are reading a podcast on your commute or searching for a new flat, these updates show how much tech companies shape our daily lives.
