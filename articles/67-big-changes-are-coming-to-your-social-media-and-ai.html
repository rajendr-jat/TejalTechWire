Big Changes Are Coming To Your Social Media And AI Tools

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 2d ago

2 massive tech updates are shaking up the digital world this week.

To start, Facebook and Instagram are getting major safety upgrades for teenagers. Meta, the parent company of both platforms, agreed to these changes to settle a nationwide US lawsuit. Soon, teens in most US states will face a strict 2 hour daily limit across both apps. They will also see pop-up reminders every 15 minutes to stop mindless scrolling. The apps will quiet down notifications between 8AM and 3PM, and block feeds between 12AM and 6AM. Additionally, teens can turn off personalized algorithms, which are the computer formulas that track your online behavior to decide what posts you see.

In other news, the business of artificial intelligence is seeing a giant shift. Computer chip giant Nvidia is in talks to buy a popular startup called Hugging Face for $12.9 billion. Hugging Face is a massive online hub where software creators share open-source AI. This term refers to AI programs with code that is public, meaning anyone can download and change it for free. Nvidia wants to support this community because it keeps more developers buying Nvidia's powerful chips. This move also helps Nvidia compete against closed AI companies that are building their own hardware.

Why does all of this matter to you? If you have teenagers in your family, their online lives are about to get much healthier and less addictive with these new controls. At the same time, the massive AI business deal could lead to more creative, cheaper, and more accessible smart tools for your daily life. Tech companies are finally working to protect young users while laying the groundwork for the next generation of software. It is a great step toward making our digital world safer and more open.
