Two exciting new tech gadgets are on the way for your wrist and your hands

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 20h ago

There are 2 major new gadgets available for pre-order right now that aim to change how we monitor our health and play video games.

This wearable is called the Luffu Link. It is a $250 health and safety wristband designed by the people who originally started Fitbit. It does not have a screen, and it comes in 3 colors. The band uses built-in LTE, which is cellular wireless technology that lets you send messages even if you do not have your phone with you. It tracks your sleep, heart rate, and steps. Users can also record quick voice notes to track their daily medication or meals. If you fall or need help, you can press a button to send your GPS location and a voice message to your family. It is designed to help people look after aging parents or family members without making them feel constantly watched. This helpful wristband will start shipping in early 2027.

Another device is a high-end portable gaming computer called the Xbox Ally X20. You can now pre-order this handheld console on its own for $1,300, and it will ship on October 15. The biggest update is its 7.4-inch OLED screen. OLED is a screen technology that offers much better contrast, making colors look extremely bright and dark areas look truly black. This model also features comfortable hand grips and a better control pad. While it is quite expensive, it is built for gamers who want the ultimate visual experience. Other older versions of this console will stay on sale for lower prices.

These new releases matter because they show how technology is becoming more specialized for our specific daily needs. Whether you want to keep your family safe with simple, screen-free tracking, or you want a top-tier visual experience for gaming on the go, there are exciting new tools on the horizon. We will have to wait and see how they perform in real life once they finally arrive.
