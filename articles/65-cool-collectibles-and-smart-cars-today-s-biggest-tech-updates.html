Cool Collectibles and Smart Cars: Today's Biggest Tech Updates

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 3d ago

This week brings 2 big tech updates, with Xbox launching a special gaming console and Volvo making its cars smart enough to warn each other about road hazards. If you love gaming or want safer roads, these new features are worth watching.

To start, Xbox is celebrating its 25th birthday with a new limited-edition console bundle that costs $900. This bundle includes a translucent Xbox Series X, which means the plastic shell of the console is see-through green. It also comes with a matching green controller and a digital copy of the classic game Halo: Campaign Evolved. Each console has its own special number engraved on it. If you want one, you can preorder it on August 27 at 10 AM ET. The bundle will ship on November 15. If $900 is too expensive, you can buy just the special green controller for $80.

On the road, Volvo is updating its electric vehicles so they can talk to each other to prevent accidents. Only 3 models, the EX90, ES90, and EX60, will use cameras and sensors to spot dangers. This includes things like wild animals, people walking, bike riders, and car crashes. When a Volvo car sees a hazard, it sends the location to Volvo's internet servers. The system then alerts other Volvo drivers heading toward that same spot. Volvo also announced that its EX90 car will support spatial audio for Apple CarPlay. Spatial audio is a technology that makes music sound like it is wrapping all around you inside the car.

These updates show how our daily devices are becoming more connected and personal. You might be playing a game on a see-through console. Or you might be driving a car that looks out for your safety. Either way, technology is finding new ways to fit into our everyday lives. It will be exciting to see how these new tools perform in the real world.
