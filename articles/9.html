How to Grab a Huge Holiday Deal and Cast Your Screen to the TV

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 13h ago

Best Buy is celebrating its 60th anniversary by offering a massive discount on its gift cards this Saturday, August 22nd. For 1 day only, you can buy a $100 gift card for just $60 at your local store. Why does this matter to you? It is a rare chance to get $40 of free money to spend on top gadgets. You can combine this gift card with other big discounts at the store, like an iPad Air M4 for $649 or premium headphones for $429. This deal is only available in physical stores while supplies last, so you should plan to arrive right when your local shop opens.

Once you bring your new Apple device home, you can use a built-in feature called AirPlay to send your favorite shows to your TV. AirPlay is a wireless tool that beams video or music from your phone or laptop directly to your television screen. To make it work, connect both your TV and your mobile device to the exact same home Wi-Fi network. Then, open a streaming app, tap the tiny icon shaped like a rectangle with a small arrow, and select your television from the list.

If your favorite website or document does not support this feature, you can use screen mirroring instead. Screen mirroring is a tool that copies everything on your phone screen onto your television in real-time, including your swipes and scrolling. To turn it on, swipe down from the top right of your phone screen and tap the button with 2 overlapping squares. Now you can easily share web pages, mobile games, or family photos with everyone in the room. It is a simple way to get the most out of your home entertainment.
