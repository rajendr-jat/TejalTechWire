Tricking Chatbots and Finding Great Budget Laptop Deals

TO
TejalTechWire Original · 11d ago

This week, we have 2 major tech updates: a trick that gets AI chatbots to write adult stories, and a new budget laptop that offers great value.

Recently, researchers found a loophole in Claude, a popular artificial intelligence chatbot. Claude has strict rules against writing explicit content. However, people found a way to "jailbreak" older versions, which means they tricked the AI into breaking its own safety rules. By using a clever conversation technique, a researcher convinced the AI that it was being unfair to female characters. The AI fell for the trick and wrote explicit sexual text. While the newest versions of Claude are safe, these older models are still online and available for people to use.

On the hardware side, there is some good news if you need a new computer. HP has released a new 16-inch laptop called the OmniBook 3 for about $520. This laptop stands out because it has 16GB of RAM. RAM is the short-term memory that helps your computer run multiple apps at the same time without slowing down. Many expensive laptops today only give you 8GB of RAM, which is not enough anymore. The OmniBook 3 has some cheap plastic parts and a basic screen, but its great memory and long battery life make it a smart buy.

These stories matter because they show how fast technology is changing. AI companies are constantly struggling to keep their software safe, while hardware makers are finding ways to give you more power for less cash. Keeping up with these changes helps you make better choices when buying gadgets and staying safe online.
